## Summary

All four phases carry `passed` VERIFICATION.md reports with live UAT rounds complete
(59: 15/15; 60: 5/5; 61: 21 rows + five wd6 rows; 62: 10/10). All four VALIDATION.md
files are Nyquist COMPLIANT. Cross-phase wiring is strong: the zoom pitch authority
threads into the motion lanes, the compositor applies exactly one transform to every
content raster (vector and curve-rebaked paint included), the canonical/Rust declaration
pair covers every Phase 59-62 field, the shared vector editing surface is not forked,
and loop-occurrence invalidation is source-scoped and motion-independent.

The audit's single critical finding — Motion-only operations are absent from the Studio
undo ledger (MOTN-12 undo half) — was **confirmed live on 2026-11-14** (Cmd+Z after
Animate / adding a Motion channel leaves the Motion in place) and **accepted by the
user as a v2.1.0 deviation**: the operation is non-destructive and the Motion channel
can be removed explicitly. 59-UAT row 11 had recorded this undo step as passing; the
recorded row was misobserved, not a later regression. The deviation and its follow-up
candidate are recorded above.
