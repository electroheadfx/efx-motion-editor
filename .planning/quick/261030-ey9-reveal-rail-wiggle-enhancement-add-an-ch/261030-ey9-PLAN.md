Add a Reveal "Photo wiggle" toggle that moves the whole revealed result (placed photo plus wash silhouette) as one rigid unit per held frame, and add Rotation and Scale as two extra channels of the existing deterministic stop-motion wiggle law for every mode (Paint Motion, Paint Static, Reveal Motion, Reveal Static), controlled from the Create Rail dialog Motion wiggle card and the right-panel Motion tab sharing one settings model.

Purpose: the Reveal rail today wiggles only the wash coverage, so the photo stays nailed to the frame while the pigment boils. The user wants the reveal to breathe as one object, and wants the wiggle family (Deformation, Position) completed with Rotation and Scale.

Output: extended held-pose law with centroid-pivoted rotation/scale, reveal post-composite rigid pose with photo-centre pivot, persisted optional `rotation`/`scale` on motion records with conditional canonical emission in TS and Rust, persisted `photoWiggle` on reveal Loop Clips, and four wiggle rows across the two control surfaces.
</objective>
