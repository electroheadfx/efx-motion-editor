<objective>
Make the Physic Paint Studio transport play a selected rail's own range.

Requirements: JQ0-01 selected-rail range on Play; JQ0-02 loop-on wrap / loop-off stop at the selection bounds; JQ0-03 no-selection full-timeline fallback.

Locked behavior (from the quick-task clarifications, non-negotiable):
1. Surface is the Studio transport (Play cached Roto frames + Loop), not the main-editor timeline.
2. "Selected rail" means the Studio rail selection: a Key Rail segment, one or more Loop Clip rails, or a rail-set selection.
3. With a rail selection, Play re-anchors to the selection's first frame, never the cursor.
4. Several selected rails play the contiguous span from the earliest selected rail start to the latest selected rail end; gaps play the normal composite.
5. Loop ON wraps to the selection start; Loop OFF stops at the selection end (the existing end-of-list stop).
6. No rail selection keeps today's full composite enumeration (0..getEndFrame-1), play-from-cursor, cursor-anchored loop wrap.
7. RANGE scoping only. Never re-introduce solo-style per-frame hiding or attribution; the global Solo (quick 261022-iff) stays a separate composite focus and must not be read here.

Purpose: the transport currently enumerates the whole composite no matter what is selected. A selected rail should audition its own start-to-end range.

Output: one pure range-derivation module with tests, one optional playback port threaded through the coordinator and consumed by the hook at Play press, and a thin Studio resolver that maps the three existing selection authorities to window bounds. Two live UAT rows cover selection playback, one covers the no-selection fallback.

Scope fences: no new dependencies; no dev-server runs; no pushes; atomic local commits; Preact + @preact/signals only (no useState); vitest `run` only (never watch). The window is session-only: never persisted, never crosses the bridge, never enters the document revision, never touches export.
