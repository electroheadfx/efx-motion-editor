## Footprint table (agreed — one law governs engine, punch and cursor)

| Tool | Radius law | Shape | Rotation |
|---|---|---|---|
| brush | `(b.size[0] + (b.size[1]-b.size[0])·pr^pressureSize) · clamp(1-speed·speedThin, 0.4, 1) · sizeMult · 0.8` | tip ellipse / rounded rect / tip mask | fixed / follow / random |
| water | brush × 1.3 × (1 + min(speed,2.5)·0.12) | same tip | same |
| pen | `(0.0016 + 0.0042·pr) · clamp(1.12 - speed·0.3, 0.55, 1.12) · sizeMult` | circle | none |
| lift | `(0.012 + 0.03·pr) · sizeMult` | tip aspect + rotation + tip mask | fixed / follow / random per tip |

`sizeMult = 3^((engineSize - 0.5)·2)` with `engineSize = toEngineParams({ size }).size`. The cursor preview evaluates every law at speed 0 (the engine's own `updateCursor` passes 0); the punch mirrors the recorded tick grid.

Fixed rotation is not the nominal preset angle alone: `Engine.brushAngle` resolves it as `s.azimuth ?? b.angle * DEG`, where the azimuth is `Math.atan2(-ty, tx)` recorded whenever `hypot(tx, ty) > 12` and held (sticky) until the next tilted sample (`Engine.ts:1552/1568`). The punch replays that per recorded point and the cursor reads the live pointer `tiltX`/`tiltY`, so engine, punch and cursor agree for a tilted pen as well as a mouse.

## Declared approximations (do not "fix" them here)

- The punch uses the RECORDED tick grid (`stroke.points`) and the engine's own angle law: `fixed` = the recorded tilt azimuth (`atan2(-ty, tx)` when `hypot(tx, ty) > 12`, sticky across ticks) falling back to the preset angle; `follow` = `atan2(dy, dx) + preset angle`; `random` = the nominal preset angle. Per-dab `angleJitter` and `random` rotation (spatter/sponge/stipple) are not reproducible from the record; a re-bake of a random-rotation tip is already RNG-divergent per session (same class).
- Bilinear tip sampling in the punch mirrors GL_LINEAR + CLAMP_TO_EDGE.
- `liftFS` keeps the pre-k03c radial envelope `exp(-(q·q)²)` and multiplies by the tip mask (`uRound = 0`): a round tip recovers today's erase exactly, any other tip shaves the silhouette to its contour. Legacy recorded lift strokes (none in practice — the k03 gate blocked every recorded lift) would re-bake tip-shaped.
- Base recovery is a ONE-TIME cost per legacy key and runs only when the map has no entry for the key; a key whose recovery failed this session is refused immediately (a ref-held failure memo), never re-baked on every gesture.
- The recovered base is validated, not proven: the ≤3/255 gate is the contract, and `meanAbsoluteChannelError` is measured over the RGB channels of the recomposition versus the displayed composite.
