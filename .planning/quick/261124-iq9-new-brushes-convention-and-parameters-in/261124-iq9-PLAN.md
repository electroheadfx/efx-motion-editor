<objective>
Deliver the new brush convention in the Studio paint surface.

A. Two media only: Watercolor and Gouache. The old Gouache medium (id `gouache`, presets Gouache Round / Gouache Flat), Chalk (pastel) and Graphite are retired outright — they disappear from the Medium dropdown and from the catalog. The Acrylic medium keeps its id (`acrylic`) and every parameter, and takes the display name **Gouache** (emboss is not a usable 2.5D relief, so "acrylic/oil" was the wrong story).

B. Twelve responses in two groups: Watercolor keeps its eleven; the acrylic group keeps exactly one — Bristle Round, suppressed-flat sibling removed, renamed **Gouache**. Every Chalk and Graphite response is deleted.

C. Viscosity and Water stop appearing for the opaque medium in the Brush tool — on EVERY visible surface, not just one: the right-side Paint panel (the Matrix-2 row law) AND the Studio top bar (the Water slider). Both read one shared selector, so they cannot disagree. Watercolor is untouched, and the Water tool keeps Viscosity.

D. Old projects lose the removed presets with no migration: a recorded response id that no longer exists resolves through the engine's shipped unknown-id fallback to `round` — the accepted data loss, with no back-compat branch and no hidden registrations.

Purpose: one honest, compact convention — two media, twelve responses, and a panel and top bar that show only the parameters the active medium actually reads.

Output: a narrowed package catalog and taxonomy with updated pins, the shared visibility selector driving BOTH the panel and the top-bar Water slider, every app test repointed to a surviving vehicle, and green package/app gates including both full suites and both typechecks.
</objective>
