<objective>
Make the pan a true Hand: free movement of the canvas on both surfaces, at Fit, at 1:1, and at every zoom between — including pushing the sheet entirely off any edge.

Purpose: today two independent clamps return a zero pan budget whenever the sheet fits its container (`resolveMaxPan` in the Physic Paint Studio, `clampPan` in the main editor). The Hand and middle-drag therefore feel dead at Fit, the state the user is in most of the time. D-01/D-02 lock the fix: free pan on BOTH canvases, no retention rule, Fit restores.

Output: three focused edits — the Studio store law (clamp deleted, sanitizer kept), the Studio middle-drag gate and doc sweep, and the main-editor store law with its first focused test. No new UI, no new signal, no dependency.
</objective>

**User decisions (locked — from the quick-task description, do not revisit):**
- **D-01:** BOTH canvases gain the freedom — the Physic Paint Studio sheet and the main editor canvas.
- **D-02:** Free pan means NO bound at all. The canvas may be pushed completely off-screen. Fit restores it. Do NOT implement a "keep N% visible" retention rule.

**The two clamps are the same formula, independently written:**
- Studio: `resolveMaxPan` → `max(0, (display - container) / 2)` per axis, i.e. zero whenever the sheet fits.
- Main editor: `clampPan` → `maxPanX = max(0, (pW - cW / z) / 2)`, the same zero at Fit and 1:1.

**Reactivity rules that bind these edits:** writes belong to handlers/store methods (never a render body); store setters are compare-then-write (rule 3); narrow signal reads stay narrow. This plan adds no effect, no computed and no render-body write.
