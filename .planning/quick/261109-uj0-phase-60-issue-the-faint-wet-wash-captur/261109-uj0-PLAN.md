<objective>
Make the blank-sheet capture gate localized so a faint wet wash can no longer be mistaken for a cleared sheet, and make every remaining rejection readable from a packaged build.

The 261109-pwt fix lowered the global floor to 0.3, and `pnpm tauri dev` passed UAT, but the compiled v2.1.0-beta2 app still rejects the same faint wash even though its embedded asset manifest carries the fixed Studio bundle and the compiled constant is 0.3. The global statistic — a mean over every one of the 2304 downsampled pixels — falls with canvas coverage and depends on when the sheet is sampled, so a legitimate faint wash can score in the blank band in one runtime and above the floor in another. The global mean cannot be made robust by lowering the floor again: the measured blank maximum is 0.05-0.12 and any further reduction erodes the blank margin.

Purpose: decide "blank or painted" from a localized signal (the densest 12x12 sampling window, with a per-pixel darkening cap so isolated specks are an area phenomenon, not an amplitude one) while keeping the historical global floor and its diagnostic meaning. Because the packaged build shows a runtime divergence that cannot be reproduced from the dev timing, every remaining rejection must print its measured values in production so the next reproduction is evidence, not guesswork.

Output: one pure measurement/gate module with behavior fixtures over raw pixel data, both Studio call sites rewired to the shared predicate, one non-DEV `console.warn` line per rejection with measured values and frame/identity context, and updated capture-gate pins. No change to the eager snapshot, the sequence assignment, the promotion/retry/settle/flush laws, the capture pipeline ordering, or any store/engine/compositor code.
</objective>
