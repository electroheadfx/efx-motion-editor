<objective>
Make a high-Body Brush stroke actually replace the paint it crosses: erase the pre-stroke mobile ink inside the engine and punch the cached base of a reopened frame, using the solid core to erase and the rim to mix.

Purpose: live UAT at Body 100 shows the covering stroke leaving every crossed colour visible, because `coverPass` only erases settled `fixed` pigment and nothing reaches `ink` or the cached base. This plan closes that seam end-to-end while leaving `Body 0`, the cover curve, scattering and relief untouched.
Output: engine `preInk` snapshot + `inkCover` subtract + `onCoverDab` emission; a `core` coverage profile in the Lift punch library; cover dispatch through the Studio's Lift preview/commit transaction; source-scan pins, a `core`-profile unit test, a cover seam contract test, and a `cover-over-wet` probe gate with a committed pre-fix baseline. No existing Lift literal, count or message changes: the cover is purely additive in every pinned window, and three deliberate shapes keep pre-existing counts outside the write set intact — the `inkCover` program binds AFTER the `this.programs = {` literal (twenty-one entries stay twenty-one), the stroke-start arming reads `this.params.body` instead of the curve (Engine.ts keeps its single `bodyCurve(` call site), and the cover commit passes a real `cachedBase` behind `baseCanvas: punched.canvas` (the whole-Studio `cachedBase: null` count stays 2).
