<objective>
Rework the Physics Paint right-sidebar Color tab palette: drop the per-swatch X button, make swatches selectable, let a committed color change rewrite the selected swatch, and delete the selected swatch with Delete/Backspace while the palette grid owns those keys.

Purpose: the tiny X buttons are hard to hit and sit outside the 32px cells; users want to pick a palette entry and edit it in place. Selection plus key deletion replaces the two-click removal flow with one deliberate key press, without letting Delete/Backspace leak into stroke/clip/layer deletion.

Output: a selectable swatch grid (`aria-pressed` + accent outline), a commit-time in-place rewrite of the selected entry (favorites / recents / hidden-default convention), grid-scoped Delete/Backspace containment, and updated behavior + source-pin suites.
</objective>
