<objective>
Swap the two empty-frame key-creation break semantics per the user-confirmed product decision: Insert always creates a CONNECTED key (no break), while Paint on an empty frame always creates a BROKEN key (persistent incoming interpolation break owned by the new key).

Purpose: The gap-starting gesture moves from Insert to Paint. Insert now extends the nearest left segment (including in trailing empty space and inside intentional gaps, where the right segment's break survives); Paint on an empty frame starts a new segment and its own Key Rail. This is a behavior-only change with no saved-project migration.
Output: A resolver break-semantics swap, a paste-key `startsNewSegment` flag, paint-on-empty and + Key routed to broken-key creation, updated Insert copy, RED tests first, and a green full suite.
</objective>
