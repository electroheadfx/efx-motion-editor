<objective>
Fold a cheap, view-fixed Blinn-Phong specular sheen into the Studio paint emboss, so impasto crests catch a soft highlight — no new passes, samplers, textures, pixel formats, or UI.

Purpose: the shipped emboss is a first-order heightmap term (diffuse only); a real impasto reference shows a second, tight highlight where the crest faces the light. ONE shared display source (`displaySource`) feeds both the shipped compositor (`displayFS` — every export and the amount-0 path) and the live stroke layer (`displayLayerFS`), so the term must live in that one block and be folded into `shade` BEFORE the single clamp: mode 2 (transmittance), mode 4 (straight-alpha pigment) and the cached-base round-trip then inherit it by construction and alpha is never touched. The highlight is therefore a pigment/transmittance LIFT (a sheen) and cannot exceed the fond in the multiply compositor — accepted for this quick; a true additive white glint is out of scope.

Output: shaders.ts (`uSpecHalf` + four named constants + the term + the doc law), Engine.ts (CPU half-vector upload, comment updates), emboss.test.ts (updated pins + the new spec law family), green package gates, and a SUMMARY whose live UAT rows are UNCLAIMED — the artist's eye in Studio is the only look gate.

Design notes (deviations from the requested literal, with reasons):
- A literal `max(dot(n, H) - H.z, 0.0)` cannot carry the 16th-power gloss: a typical crest delta (≤0.2) raised to 16 is ~1e-12 and the sheen would never render. The zero subtraction therefore happens AFTER the exponent — `max(pow(max(dot(n, H), 0.0), g) - pow(H.z, g), 0.0)`. The flat-normal identity stays EXACT: on a flat field n is exactly (0,0,1), so `dot(n, H)` evaluates to `H.z` bit-exactly and both `pow` operands are one evaluation whose difference is 0. This is the same zero-gradient-subtraction law the diffuse term states with `dot(n, L) - L.z`.
- `pow(H.z, g)` is NOT precomputed on the CPU: GPU vs CPU `pow` can differ by 1 ulp and would break the exact flat-zero identity.
- The four relief taps and the centre tap already sampled in the emboss block are reused as-is (no extra fetch); the half-vector is the only new input and it is CPU-built once per draw.
- Per-medium gloss (watercolor matte vs acrylic/gouache gloss) is DEFERRED: the engine has no medium identity today and this quick adds none; one global gain/gloss serves every medium.
- Accepted tradeoff: because the term only lifts pigment/transmittance, the highlight cannot read brighter than the fond under a multiply compositor. A true additive white glint needs a different compositing route and is out of scope.

1. H = normalize(L + V) with V = (0,0,1), built in `Engine.drawDisplay` from the radians light, uploaded as ONE vec3 (`uSpecHalf`). No per-fragment `normalize`.
