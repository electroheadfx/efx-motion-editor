## Phase Goal

**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>
Establish the production-only pre-UAT physical contract foundation with stable `keyId` plus direct physical `appFrame`, enabled-only interpolation state, runtime-only generated cells, and a separate Script Motion contract. Keep the current shared live timing contract compile-preserving and solely active until the owning consumer plans perform coherent cutovers; remove compatibility and migration paths during those cutovers rather than layering an adapter. Regression files and Vitest remain untouched through approval of 36.14-12.

Purpose: Give Plans 36.14-02 onward one small, closed, runtime-validated physical interface they can adopt directly while preventing Plan 01 from breaking unmigrated consumers or creating two active timing authorities.

Output: One isolated physical Roto model module containing the canonical identity/settings interfaces, native key-ID creation seam, immutable defaults, and strict collection/value validators. Shared transport, project, persistence, store, Studio, and Script consumers remain unchanged in this plan and are cut over by their owning later plans before Plan 11 removes all legacy members.
</objective>
