## Phase Goal

**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>
Cut the store, hydration, layer initialization, selection, and shared workflow callers over to physical records and runtime-derived cells so stored, selected, displayed, and reopened positions share one coordinate without source/display projection or durable generated timing truth.

Purpose: Establish the physical current-state boundary consumed by the acknowledged coordinator and downstream parity plans. After this plan, production callers can read and select real keys by stable identity, navigate one direct physical frame space, and render exact runtime interiors without retaining the removed timing model in store or presentation state.

Output: One shared physical projection seam, a physical-record store boundary, atomic physical launch hydration, identity-based Signals selection, and direct physical workflow/session/editing ports.
</objective>
