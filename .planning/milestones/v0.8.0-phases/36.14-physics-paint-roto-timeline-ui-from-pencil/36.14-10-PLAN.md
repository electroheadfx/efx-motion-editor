<objective>

Purpose: Complete every mutable and visible downstream consumer omitted from Plan 09. A moved, deleted, reopened, undone, or redone key must carry its own payload by stable identity, reject stale async pixels, publish dirty/visual state exactly once, and render everywhere at its accepted direct physical position.

Output: One pure identity ownership rebuild, stable live-pixel revision gates, canonical dirty/visual publication, physical traversal, one shared Roto preview/export renderer, deterministic missing/background composition, physical timeline extent, and deletion of the legacy cache transaction module.
</objective>
