Replay provenance supplies command ID, direction, source revision, and target revision. `RotoPhysicalEditSnapshot` already carries launchOperationId, layerId, projectContextId, capacity, records, interpolation, Loop Clips, incoming breaks, selectedKeyId, selectedAppFrame, currentAppFrame, buffers, and engine state.
