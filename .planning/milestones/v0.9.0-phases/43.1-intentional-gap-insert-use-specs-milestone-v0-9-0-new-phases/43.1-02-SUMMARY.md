## Decisions Made

- Break ownership is never frame-owned or span-owned; only the right real key's stable ID decides whether its incoming adjacency projects generated cells.
- A resolver proposal uses `nextIncomingInterpolationBreakKeyIds: null` for unchanged ownership and a complete frozen replacement only for empty-segment insertion or owner deletion.
- The empty-segment resolver does not infer intent from generic paste or `insert-slot`; it validates one explicit operation with a supplied blank payload and fresh ID.
- Loop Clip frames are classified through the existing compact loop derivation and lazy per-frame resolver, avoiding a second linked-frame authority or duration-proportional materialization.
