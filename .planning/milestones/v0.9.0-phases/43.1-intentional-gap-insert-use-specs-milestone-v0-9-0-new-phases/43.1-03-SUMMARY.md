## Decisions Made

- The complete owner array is the only coordinator/history authority; a nullable proposal member means ownership is unchanged, while replay snapshots always win over proposals and current state.
- Record and owner replacement ordering is selected from current and target referential validity. When neither direct order is valid, owners are cleared, records replaced, then target owners installed before any transaction can settle.
- Matching acceptance requires the result's complete owner collection, semantic delta, staged revision, and operation tuple to equal the pending transaction exactly.
- Break-only differences are history-bearing canonical changes; selection/cursor-only or other untracked derived differences remain no-change outcomes.
