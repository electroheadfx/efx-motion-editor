## Decisions Made

- Finite records without lifecycle fields hydrate at the canonical parser seam to synchronized, attached, contiguous semantics. Legacy infinity records remain unsynthesized because no accepted finite extent exists from which to derive `originalEndExclusive`; explicit infinity lifecycle records remain valid.
- Group gaps remain represented only by normalized `visibleRanges`. No deletion mask, deleted-frame set, fragment identity, Group break set, or blank-key sentinel was introduced.
- `incomingInterpolationBreakKeyIds` remains a separate real-key interpolation authority and does not encode Group gaps.
- Exact-frame Group Paint overrides remain stable `keyId` references to canonical real keys. Persistence stores those real-key raster payloads in existing PNG sidecars and never embeds raster bytes in Group records.
- No production store change was necessary: the existing deep replacement module already met the complete-map, global-reference-pruning, and exact-once notification contract.
