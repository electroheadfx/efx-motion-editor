## Decisions Made

- The final Group target proposal is recomputed from `records.getDocument()` only after the canonical lease is acquired; pre-flush target identity is not treated as publication authority.
- `incomingInterpolationBreakKeyIds` remains byte-identical during Group gap refill because `visibleRanges` is the sole deleted-occurrence authority.
- Group Paint preserves the current stable selection/cursor while using the exact painted app frame as the transport settlement frame.
- Deferred operations explicitly record whether local staging occurred, preventing rejection, timeout, cancellation, or disposal from producing a false version/cache publication.
