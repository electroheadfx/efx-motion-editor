## Decisions Made

- Kept Action provenance and source ownership independent: two Groups may originate from the same Action while owning independent source cycles.
- Required explicit sharing identity plus identical ordered source key cycles; reordered cycles are invalid rather than silently reinterpreted.
- Computed cleanup from the complete surviving candidate document, including both cycle source keys and exact-frame override keys.
- Modeled physical Paint as override-first copy-on-write so raster mutation can never leak through a shared source identity.
- Required revision and content-hash agreement for history and recovery publication, in addition to exact canonical lease ownership.
- Treated rejected proposals as true no-ops by preserving the exact accepted ledger object and recording zero publication events.
