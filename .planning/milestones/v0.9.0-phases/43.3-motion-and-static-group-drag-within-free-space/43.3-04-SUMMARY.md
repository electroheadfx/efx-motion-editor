### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Correctness] Rightward ghost preview collapsed to a 1px sliver**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (native UAT)
- **Issue:** `updateGhost` anchored the ghost's right edge at the original `effectiveEnd` and shrank the width as the destination moved right; once `destination >= effectiveEnd` the width collapsed to `Math.max(1, ...)` = 1px, making the rightward ghost invisible while leftward grew.
- **Fix:** Width is now the group's constant resolved width `(effectiveEnd - placementStart) x pitch`, rigidly translated by the drag delta. Presentation-only; no resolver settlement or accepted movement behavior changed.
- **Files modified:** `app/src/components/physic-paint/hooks/usePhysicsPaintGroupRailDrag.ts`, `app/src/components/physic-paint/view/PhysicsPaintLoopClipRail.test.tsx`
- **Verification:** New symmetric leftward/rightward regression (RED before fix, GREEN after); full suite 2004 passed; independent review APPROVE.
- **Committed in:** `1f0e41c2` (RED), `628d4618` (GREEN)
