**Determination (2026-08-13): PRE-EXISTING baseline debt — NOT introduced or exposed by Phase 43.3.**

- **Reproduces on the exact pre-43.3 baseline `38cf2448`:** the deterministic regression (lifecycle-available Group, `repeat: 'infinity'`, stale `originalEndExclusive`/`visibleRanges`) fails identically on the baseline — `effectiveEnd` = 12 (stale source-cycle end) instead of 30 (parent end). Verified empirically in a `38cf2448` worktree.
- **Root cause (both files byte-identical between `38cf2448` and HEAD):**
  1. `updateLoop` in `physicsPaintRotoPlayScriptController.ts` guards the lifecycle rebuild with `if (draftRepeat !== 'infinity' …)` — setting Repeat to Infinity keeps the stale `originalEndExclusive`/`visibleRanges`.
  2. `derivePhysicPaintRotoLoopRanges` in `physicsPaintRotoPhysicalResolver.ts` uses `originalEndExclusive` for lifecycle-available clips, ignoring `repeat: 'infinity'` — the range stays pinned to the source cycle.
- **Affected layer:** canonical resolver (range derivation) + the loop-edit apply path. Timeline and canvas both consume the same `context.ranges`, so both show no derived occurrences.
- **Regression anchor added:** `physicsPaintRotoPhysicalResolver.test.ts` — "resolves Infinity repeat occurrences through the accepted boundary for a lifecycle-available Group (baseline contract)" (currently RED on HEAD, matching the baseline).
