## Decisions Made

- The clamp's authoritative right boundary is `min(capacity, parentEndExclusive)`, so preview and commit cannot place any member beyond the accepted parent sequence even when storage capacity is larger.
- Delete Key Rail uses a dedicated absolute-position survivor mapping rather than the legacy `delete-key-group` left-ripple candidate. This follows the plan's required persistent vacated interval and exact surviving positions while leaving the locked Group branch byte-unchanged.
- Exact segment membership is checked with the shared `deriveKeyRailSegments` projection, avoiding a second interpretation of break and Group ownership boundaries.
