## Decisions Made

- Key Rails are presentation-only derived data. Segment membership is recomputed from canonical real-key ordering, accepted break owners, and Group ownership.
- A break is owned by the right-hand stable key and splits immediately before that key; the resolver replaces the complete break-owner collection rather than emitting a delta.
- The action layer reclassifies at activation time. Rendered availability is advisory, while the activation snapshot remains authoritative.
- Existing-break activation is an exact no-op before the shared runner, so no status or history artifact can be created.
- Scissor has a dedicated `SquareSplitHorizontal` glyph and no keyboard shortcut, preserving the existing Cut contract.
