**User-locked edge cases (free-text):**
1. Cmd+Shift+click ADDS the anchor→clicked range to the current set (union — Finder behavior); anchor does not move.
2. After batch Move commits, the whole set stays selected at the new positions; anchor follows rail identity; Undo restores the set at original positions; Redo re-applies at destination.
3. After Delete Rails commits, selection is empty, cursor stays on current valid frame; one Undo restores the exact pre-delete selection set; Redo clears again; anchor follows rail identity across Undo/Redo.
