## Cross-Phase Integration

**Score: 16/17 seams wired · 8/9 E2E flows complete.**

### Findings

**BLOCKER — display-class edits dropped by the save change-token** (PKG-06, BKG-09, REF-05 adjacency)

`buildEfxPaintLayerChangeTokenValue` (efxPaintPersistence.ts:521-527) is `` `${documentRevision}+${compositeRevision}|audios:…` ``. It has no `|bgT:` and no photo-display term. The display setters — `setBackgroundTransform` (efxPaintStore.ts:1249), `setBackgroundTransformLocked` (:1279), `_setPhotoReferenceDisplayProperty` (:1106) — explicitly do not bump `documentRevision` ("display prefs are not `buildEfxPaintDocumentRevision` terms").

After a settled `packageFileTokens` baseline, a display-only edit yields `changedTokens.size === 0` (efxPaintPersistence.ts:1149) → the empty-change branch returns early → `layers/<layerId>.json` is never rewritten (:1181-1183) → close/reopen silently reverts the edit. **First save of a session masks the bug** (empty baseline always writes), which is why the rd4 4-row UAT and `efxPaintBackgroundTransform.test.ts` (5/5) pass — they pin the sync/save *fingerprints*, not the layer write gate. The `|bgT:` term exists only on `buildEfxPaintSaveFingerprint` (efxPaintPersistence.ts:612-634), whose sole production caller is the derived-frame cache leg (:722).

Reproduction: open project → any token-rotating edit → save (baseline settles) → drag Background transform (or toggle its lock, or change photo-reference opacity/visibility/transform) → save/autosave → no-op → reopen → edit gone.

Fix shape: append `|bgT:` / `|photoDisplay:` terms to the layer change token (same precedent as the existing `|audios:` term), or route display setters through a token-rotating path.

**WARNING — export mix silently skips undecoded document-sound buffers** (SND-07, SND-06)

Decode at open is fire-and-forget (projectStore.ts:778-799); export never awaits clip buffers (exportEngine.ts:334-375); `audioExportMixer.ts:101-102` does `if (!buffer) continue`. A moved/failed/undecoded clip is dropped from the exported mix with no per-track diagnostic while any other buffer exists (only a zero-buffers console warning). Practical window is small (export renders all frames before the audio step) — hence warning, not blocker.

**WARNING — two independent hide/solo truth tables** (CMP-02)

`participatingPaintTracks` (efxPaintHideSolo.ts:30) serves compositor/export; `resolvePhysicPaintTrackVisibility` (previewRenderer.ts:93) serves Studio preview. Logic-identical today and separately pinned, but no shared import enforces parity — a future edit to one desyncs preview from export.

**WARNING — main playback silently no-ops on undecoded document sound** (SND-03, SND-05 adjacency)

`audioEngine.play` returns silently when the buffer is absent (audioEngine.ts:49,:109) while open-time decode is fire-and-forget (projectStore.ts:778-799) — a play immediately after opening the project drops document-sound audio with no diagnostic. Found by the 2026-10-12 integration re-check; same family as the export-mix warning above (`playbackEngine.ts:319-355` itself gates and mixes correctly).

**INFO — no numbered REQ covers photo-display / background-transform persistence.** BKG-09 enumerates clips/order/IDs/repeats/gaps/fallback/effective rendering; REF-05 covers source identity and mode. Transform/display prefs are covered only by the rd4 quick contract and design law D-11 — and that contract's mechanism is the broken one.

**INFO — Phase 51 has no PLAN/SUMMARY artifacts** (quicks-delivered). Evidence chain adequate (51-VERIFICATION 4/4) but non-standard.

## Verdict

The v1.0.0 milestone is **structurally complete and shipped**, but this audit found one post-release defect that falsifies part of its definition of done:

1. **Requirements:** 65/68 satisfied. **PKG-06 and BKG-09 are partially unsatisfied** — the save/reopen promise holds for content-class edits but **drops display-class edits** (background transform/lock, photo-reference display prefs) after the first save of a session. SND-07 is partial on the export-mix edge only.
2. **Phases:** 14/14 verified. Three `human_needed` verdicts are non-blocking (46 GO; 47 UI-UAT deferred-and-covered; 52.1 D-11 user-accepted).
3. **Integration:** 16/17 seams wired, 8/9 E2E flows complete. The single blocker is the layer change-token field drop.
4. **Release:** v1.0.0 is public and Latest, signed/notarized, downloaded-artifact verified. The defect is post-release and does not invalidate the artifact — it is a save-path data-loss bug reachable in ordinary use (edit transform after the first save).
5. **Nyquist:** 14/14 compliant (Phase 53 promoted to `validated` after the 2026-10-11 audit).
6. **Tech debt:** substantial but non-blocking — accepted review warnings (WR/F), 52.4 supersession overrides, 52.1 D-11 accepted risk, WINDOWS.md open items including the paperPass partial-alpha wash (#79).

**The FAIL gate fires** (unsatisfied requirements). The defect is narrow (one token term missing), has a clear fix shape (the `|audios:` precedent on the same token), and does not affect the already-published bytes' core multi-track/reveal/export promises.
