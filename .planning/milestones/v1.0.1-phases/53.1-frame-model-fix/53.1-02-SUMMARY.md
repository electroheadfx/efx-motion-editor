## Decisions Made

- **D-03 single resolver honored:** `resolveOverlaySample` is the only hold/loop/skip branch. The returned `localFrame` is used uniformly for every kind that carries one (including `hold-last`) — no caller recomputes the hold index.
- **D-04 untrimmed representation:** one field, two states, no flag, no sentinel. Absent `outFrame` = untrimmed = follows N. Present `outFrame` is authoritative — past out = nothing. `createFxSequence` keeps its explicit `outFrame` default (FX clips are authored with a range; the untrimmed-follows-N law is the content-overlay law).
- **Roto-extended effective out:** `buildOverlaySampleClip` feeds `getTimelineOverlaySequenceOutFrame` as the clip's outFrame when `seq.outFrame` is present, so a trimmed FX clip whose physic-paint display runs past the authored out still renders its roto content (pre-existing law, preserved). Untrimmed stays absent so it follows N.
- **Fractional frames for motion-blur:** the resolver accepts finite `globalFrame` (not just integers) so motion-blur sub-frame video seeks keep their offset (`16.5 % 16 = 0.5` under LOOP). Fail-closed still rejects NaN/Infinity.
- **sourceFrameCount at the export gate:** image-sequences supply `imageIds.length`; video duration is resolved at seek time in `previewRenderer`; FX generators have no source limit (`MAX_SAFE_INTEGER`). Past-source extrapolation is inactive for video in this plan — 53.1-03 feeds the real count and the `Sequence.extrapolation` field.
