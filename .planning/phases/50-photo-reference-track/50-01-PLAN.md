<objective>
Replace the `EfxPaintDocument.photoReference: null` placeholder with a real `PhotoReferenceTrack` model, extend the fail-closed parser to accept it, and extend the canonical revision encoder to emit real photo terms — the clean-break format change under the v1.0.0 contract. This is the model half only: no store ops, no UI, no ghost draw in this plan.

Purpose: the document is the durable store; save/reopen persists it via the Tauri filesystem. Today the parser rejects any non-null `photoReference` (efxPaintDocumentParsers.ts:327-329) and the revision builder emits a `'photo:null;'` placeholder (efxPaintDocumentRevision.ts:117). Without this extension, Plans 50-02 (store CRUD) and 50-03..50-05 (UI) have nothing to persist or resolve.

Output: `PhotoReferenceTrack` model + `parsePhotoReferenceTrack` fail-closed branch + `encodeCanonicalPhotoReference` term, with a round-trip + rejection + revision-stability contract suite.
</objective>
