## Decisions Made

- Kept selection and cursor out of the canonical revision because ordinary navigation must not alter durable document identity; exact replay snapshots independently authorize these fields.
- Performed the live source read only when Undo/Redo is invoked, so navigation does not clear history while stale replay still fails closed.
- Kept stack ownership in refs and availability/output in existing Signals, adding no mirrored `useState`, render-local Signal, or effect-driven internal synchronization.
- Revoked unreachable parent ledger entries at successful launch activation rather than allowing old operation IDs to remain usable after project, launch, or capacity replacement.
