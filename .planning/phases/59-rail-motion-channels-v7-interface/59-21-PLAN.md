<objective>
Make the Motion value inspector honest about channels the rail does not carry (gap G1's IN-02 half):
mark absent channels in the projection and render their fields visibly non-editable, with the same
sentence the command layer uses when it refuses the write.

Purpose: `projectRailMotionInspector` projects all five channels unconditionally and the panel renders
a live `NumericStepper` for each, but `writeRailMotionChannelValueAtCursor` refuses every channel not
present on the record. Before 59-17 the add-channel affordance was unreachable, so Scale, Rotation and
Opacity were permanent error fields; after 59-17 they are reachable but still look editable before the
user adds them. The inspector must show which channels exist without offering a write that can only be
refused (MUI-06: unavailable actions are disabled with a real visible reason).

Gap closed: G1 — the IN-02 half the gap names (`physicsPaintKeyRailPresentation.ts` projects all five
channels even when absent; those fields render editable then reject every write).

Wave note: this plan is the second slice of the revision-mode split of 59-17. It depends on 59-17 for
the same reason the checker asked for a declared dependency, and it touches none of 59-18's,
59-19's or 59-20's files, so its parallel wave-2 placement leaves their ordering and dependencies
unchanged.
</objective>
