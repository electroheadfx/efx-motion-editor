<objective>
Keep paint on every motion-blur sub-frame: resolve content at the integer frame, evaluate the rail
transform at the fractional frame, and never let a fractional delivery touch an integer cache entry.

Purpose: today `_resolveFlattenedFrame` rejects any non-integer frame outright, so an export at
`globalFrame + 0.5` delivers nothing and the paint layer vanishes on every sub-frame. REL-04 is the
exact contract that breaks: "integer content raster with a fractional transform". MOTN-08 then
requires the transform used at export to be the transform used in edit view and Play — same
evaluator, same inputs, only a different frame value.
</objective>
