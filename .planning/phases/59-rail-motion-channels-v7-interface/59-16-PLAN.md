<objective>
Give every motion channel a numeric editor: Position X/Y in px, Scale in %, Rotation in degrees and
Opacity in %, driven by one projection and writing through the same command layer as the gizmo.

Purpose: the lane surface edits keys in time and the gizmo edits Position/Scale/Rotation by
gesture; nothing yet edits Opacity, nothing enters an exact value, and nothing shows what a key
actually holds. Without this plan a user can create a motion key and never read or correct its
value. Grouped Position is a display grouping over two model channels that share key frames — the
values stay independent, and the inspector must not invent a coupling the model does not have.
</objective>
