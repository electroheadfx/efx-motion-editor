<objective>
Apply rail motion at the compositor: one affine transform per animated track per frame, drawn at both
existing draw sites, pivoting on a memoized rail-space ink-bounds centre, with the flattened cache
invalidated by motion revision but never by sampled values.

Purpose: MOTN-07/MOTN-08 require one raster per track per frame with the transform applied at draw
time so edit view, Play and export cannot disagree. Applying it anywhere else (a per-renderer
transform, an export-side scale) makes the three surfaces independently testable and therefore
guaranteed to drift. REL-03's second clause lives here too: the moment an evaluated value enters a
cache key, the cache stops being a content cache.
</objective>
