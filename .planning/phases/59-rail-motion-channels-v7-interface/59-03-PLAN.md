<objective>
Land rail-motion persistence atomically: the document allowlist and parser, the conditional canonical
term, the single revision-building authority in the store, and a save→reopen byte-identical pin that
covers every persistence carrier.

Purpose: this is the plan that REL-02 and REL-03 stand or fall on. A motion collection that parses
but is absent from the revision silently serves stale composites; a collection that is in the
revision but not in the runtime read silently disappears on reopen. The defect class already cost
PKG-06 and BKG-09.
</objective>
