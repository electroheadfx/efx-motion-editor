<objective>
Expose motion editing on the canvas and in the inspector: a Position/Scale/Rotation gizmo on the
selected animated rail with the D-08 gate, and numeric fields for all four channels with unbounded
rotation.

Purpose: the lane surface edits keys in time; nothing edits VALUES. Without this plan a user can
create a motion key and never give it a value. The D-08 gate is the whole safety story: a transform
gesture must never silently create animation on a rail or channel the user did not animate.
</objective>
