<objective>
Give motion its commands: Animate the selected rail, add/remove/step a Motion key, set Hold/Ease and
Wiggle on Motion keys only, keep the three selection scopes independent with no silent no-ops, and
surface a merge that drops rail motion through a session-only confirmation row.

Purpose: 59-01 through 59-11 built identity, model, storage, evaluation, compositing and a surface.
Nothing in them can create a motion key. This plan is the only writer, and it is also the only place
the D-08 "never mutate silently" rule can be enforced — a command that cannot act must say why.
</objective>
