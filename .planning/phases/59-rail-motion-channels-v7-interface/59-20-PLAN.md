<objective>
Close the reconciler's reporting and door gaps: a merge that drops rail motion must be announced
honestly, and every reveal-side direct door must reconcile motion in its own transaction.

Purpose: `_applyRotoTrackSelectionRemoval` computes the reconciliation and then keeps only
`.railMotions`, so `droppedRailId`/`droppedRailFrames` never reach a caller and the merge-drop row
can never fire on the move/cut/clear path (CR-02). On the reveal side, `deleteRevealRail`,
`resizeRevealRail` and `commitRevealBake` write records, loops and breaks directly and never touch
the motion collection, so motion survives an edit whose rails are gone or describes rails that no
longer exist (WR-04).

Gap closed: G4 — CR-02 (merge report discarded) and WR-04 (reveal delete/resize and the reveal bake
bypass motion reconciliation).

Wave note: this plan edits `physicPaintStore.ts`, which 59-19 also edits, so it runs after 59-19.
</objective>
