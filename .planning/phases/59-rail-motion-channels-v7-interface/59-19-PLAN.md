<objective>
Make an infinite Loop Clip's motion span reach the real end of the rail instead of stopping after one occurrence.

Purpose: `railViewOccurrences('infinity')` returns 1, and no consumer extends the resulting view. `_railMotionViewAtFrame` is half-open over that truncated span, so `_trackMotionTransformAtFrame` returns null for every frame past the first cycle: an infinite rail keeps cycling its content while its transform silently disappears. The same truncated view drives the motion lane width and the gizmo's cursor-inside-span gate, so the lane covers only the first occurrence and the gizmo switches off mid-rail. D-16 requires motion to hold past its last key on every occurrence; MOTN-06 and MOTN-07 both depend on the view covering the resolved span.

Gap closed: G2 — CR-04 (infinite Loop Clip motion span truncated to one occurrence).

Wave note: this plan edits `physicPaintStore.ts`, `PhysicsPaintTrackRow.tsx` and `useRotoTimelineActions.ts`, all of which 59-17 and 59-18 also edit, so the three plans cannot share a wave; 59-18 lands first.
</objective>
