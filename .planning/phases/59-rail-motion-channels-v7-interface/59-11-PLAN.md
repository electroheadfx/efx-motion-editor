<objective>
Integrate the motion block into the timeline: a session-only chevron toggle (collapsed after Animate,
one open block at a time), mirrored header rows, a measured row layout, and a reorder indicator that
respects blocks.

Purpose: the surface built in 59-10 has to live inside a strip whose every row was 31px. Adding lanes
makes rows variable height, which silently breaks three things at once: the content height, the
header/lane scroll alignment, and the reorder insertion index. All three must move to one measured
layout derivation in this plan, or the first expanded rail corrupts the strip.
</objective>
