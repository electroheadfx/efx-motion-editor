<objective>
Finish the strip conversion: the sound leaves, the viewport getter, the open/reveal positioning and
the two rail gap seams. Then retire the legacy strip pitch constant so the authority is the only
pitch definition the timeline reads.

Purpose: these are the last strip consumers that still multiply the old constant. The rail gap is
the subtle one — a rail that scales its pitch but keeps a 2px gap is 1.5px short at 0.25× and 6px
long at 4×, which a pitch-only test would never catch.

Output: every strip consumer reads the authority, the strip contains no pitch constant, and both
rail types subtract the scaled gap.
</objective>
