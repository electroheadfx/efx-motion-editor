<objective>
Prove the whole zoom architecture end-to-end on the thinnest real path: one session-only pitch
authority module, driven by Cmd/Ctrl+wheel and a header pill, feeding the ruler, the cells grid, the
lane extent, the two rail props and the playhead inside the real Studio timeline.

Purpose: ZOOM-05 is a single-authority refactor of 66 frame→pixel sites. The highest-risk failure
is not "how to zoom" — the canvas zoom store already ships — it is picking an architecture that
every remaining consumer can adopt without a second pitch definition. This plan lands that
architecture and proves it on the render path before any interaction consumer is touched.

Output: `physicsPaintTimelineZoom.ts` with its unit proof, the header pill, the modifier-wheel
branch with a one-frame coalescer, the reset seam, and the first converted consumers. The legacy
strip pitch constant stays in place as the temporary source for the interaction sites that
60-02/60-03 convert next; every consumer this plan touches reads the authority instead.
</objective>
