<objective>
Convert the two vertical surfaces that still hard-code a pitch: the non-active track rows and the
motion-lane block. Every row cell, key rail, loop clip rail, insertion preview and motion lane must
be laid out by the pitch the strip hands down, and the row's module-private pitch constant must
disappear.

Purpose: these are the last files that own a local pitch. A row drawn at 18px under a 4× ruler is
off by 54px per frame at frame 1 and diverges from the active lane it sits beside, so the row is
where a single-authority miss would be most visible.

Output: `PhysicsPaintTrackRow.tsx` declares no pitch constant, `TimelineMotionChannels.tsx` and
`TimelineMotionKeyDrag.tsx` read pitch and gap only from required props, and both consumers are
proved at 2× and 4×.
</objective>
