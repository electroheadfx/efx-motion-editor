<objective>
Convert the strip's pointer-driven surface — every projection, ghost and preview — onto the single
pitch authority, so dragging, pushing, resizing and hit-testing agree with the ruler and playhead at
every zoom level.

Purpose: the render path landed in 60-01 makes the timeline *look* right at any scale. This plan
makes it *behave* right: a drag of N frames must move N frames at 4×, and a ghost must cover the
same frames as the rail it previews. These are the sites most likely to be missed, because they are
scattered across a 6,800-line component and none of them render on the initial paint.

Output: the interaction consumers read the live pitch at event time; the cross-track drag port
receives the scaled pitch with no second multiplier; the push-projection pins are repointed.
</objective>
