<objective>
Close G-01: make a finite lifecycle (converted Standard) cycle's stored extent and paint-sheet collection follow the surviving source cycle, on read and on write, so deleted frames render empty immediately and after reopen and a repeat change can never resurrect cached paint.

Purpose: a converted Standard rail stores phaseOrigin, originalEndExclusive, and visibleRanges at conversion time (D-08..D-11). Nothing reconciles that frozen extent against the current source-key positions afterwards: derivePhysicPaintRotoLoopRanges takes naturalEnd from the stored end (:5575-5577), classifyPhysicPaintRotoGroupFrameTarget uses the stored end as the containing extent (:358-366), groupOverrideRecords are never pruned by the plain delete paths (the resolver has no groupOverrideRecords reference), and updateLoop only refuses a contraction that would drop an override rather than dropping stale ones. The UAT state (keyless gap at 1-2, override sheets at 3,4,6,8,11,12, loopClips []) is the observable result: baked sheets outlive the frames they describe, and the cycle keeps covering frames whose content is gone.

Output: one shared source-extent helper plus a reconcile applied at the coordinator write choke point (lazy repair for already-persisted documents), the same bound used by derivation, group classification, group lifecycle proposers, and updateLoop, and frame-based delete ownership parity with a locked refusal copy.

Cites: G-01 (58-UAT.md); Phase 58 D-08..D-11 (conversion), D-15 (all other guards unchanged), D-16 (locked copy), D-17 (regression locking); RAIL-03, RAIL-07.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
- Noun: the persisted Group lifecycle extent and its override collection
- Decision: chosen — a finite lifecycle clip's effective end is bounded by phaseOrigin + cycleLength * repeat computed from its live source-key positions, capacity-clamped; an override survives only while a referencing clip covers its frame; stale state persists only until the next accepted write, which repairs it atomically
- Rationale: the approved G-01 acceptance clauses require the cycle to stop at the surviving source extent, forbid orphaned paint sheets, and forbid cached empty cells on repeat changes. The orchestrator chose a shared write-and-resolve invariant over a one-off load migration, so already-orphaned persisted state is neutralized at resolution time and physically pruned by the next accepted edit. No .mce shape change; the parser stays a faithful decoder.
</assumption_delta_decision>
