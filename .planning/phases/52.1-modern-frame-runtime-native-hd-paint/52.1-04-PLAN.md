<objective>
Introduce the byte-budgeted LRU (D-08..D-12) as the single decoded-frame cache authority, make project open refs-only (lazy), and absorb the decode-once caches into the LRU. Decoded frames become ImageBitmap handles (D-13) with the premultiplyAlpha watch item (D-14).

Purpose: This is D-17 step (3) "lazy load + LRU" — the memory-stability core. The 512 MB byte budget (D-09) and pinning rule (D-11) are what keep a 100+ frame HD sequence from blowing the heap, and the single-cache-authority rule (D-12) removes the parallel registries.

Output: `frameLru.ts` (byte-budgeted LRU + pinning), refs-only `loadEfxPaintDocuments`, and the absorbed compositor/roto caches.
</objective>

|----------|-------------|
| JS (invoke) → Rust command | Untrusted frame bytes and dimensions cross the IPC boundary |
| LRU → compositor draw | ImageBitmap handles cross into drawImage; use-after-close is the risk |
