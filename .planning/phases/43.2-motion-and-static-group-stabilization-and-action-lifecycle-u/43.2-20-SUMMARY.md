## Decisions Made

- Kept referenced Action replay on a separate route from ordinary physical snapshot replay so a durable cross-resource command cannot accidentally fall through to direct local document restoration.
- Required the durable Rust committed record and accepted bridge settlement before moving `applied` or `redo` pointers; pending or rejected replay leaves history byte-identical.
- Stored the opaque script-library capability on the immutable history command because release or replay may happen after the active launch context changes.
- Preserved retained ownership beyond visible stack membership: a failed release remains queued under the exact original command/generation/reason until Rust accepts an idempotent retry.
