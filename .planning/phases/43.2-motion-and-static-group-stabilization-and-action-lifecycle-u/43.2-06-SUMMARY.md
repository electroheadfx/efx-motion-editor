## Decisions Made

- Cycle continuity belongs to immutable `phaseOrigin`; splitting `visibleRanges` never restarts a Group cycle.
- Deleted Group occurrences are represented only by range absence. `incomingInterpolationBreakKeyIds` continues to describe real-key interpolation ownership and is changed only when its owning real key is removed.
- Parent acceptance compares the independently recomputed semantic impact, records, Groups, interpolation breaks, interpolation state, selection, cursor, and canonical revision before one store replacement.
- The synchronous bridge binds the prepared Action revision exactly into recomputation; the prepared Action transaction remains responsible for the live Action-row revision lookup before submission.
- Lifecycle state remains explicit canonical provenance. History never infers synchronization or attachment from rendered pixel equality.
