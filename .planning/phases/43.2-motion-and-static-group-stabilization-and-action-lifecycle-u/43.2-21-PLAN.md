## Diagnosed gap boundary

The initiating cause is settled: `handleSelectRotoLoopClip` clears session-local multi-key selection but leaves the ordinary `selectedKeyId` Signal and canonical `PhysicPaintRotoPhysicalDocument.selectedKeyId` untouched. The Studio cursor can then advance to a Group-owned frame while the canonical document remains selected on another real key. `useRotoPhysicalEditCoordinator` correctly rejects before proposal when canonical `selectedKeyId`/`cursorAppFrame` differs from the Studio-local selection/cursor. That rejection preserves the already split authority, so a later Delete Group attempt also fails. Lease or pending leakage, cached dialog proposal, structural revision drift, and parent transaction state are eliminated as initiating causes; do not redesign those systems speculatively.
