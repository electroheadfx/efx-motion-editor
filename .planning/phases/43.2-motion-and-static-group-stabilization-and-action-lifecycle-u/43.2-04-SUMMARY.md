## Decisions Made

- Kept proposal construction entirely pure: it owns no store, Signal, version, history, cache, selection, rendering, DOM, filesystem, lease, or publication state.
- Used override-first exact-frame copy-on-write so repeated occurrences retain shared source-cycle bytes while only the painted occurrence receives a unique real-key override.
- Required the parent bridge to recompute the candidate and impact from accepted authority instead of trusting child-provided semantic claims.
- Made the store replacement the sole token-enforced publication point, with replay prevention and lease release only after complete settlement.
