# Phase 43.3 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for dragging a Motion Group or Static Group directly from its integrated Group Rail into free timeline space. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

**Primary authority:** `43.3-CONTEXT.md` D-01..D-20, gathered 2026-08-13. Supporting authority: `.planning/phases/43-hold-loop-clips-filmstrip-capsule/43-UI-SPEC.md` (accepted rail visual/interaction contract this phase extends), `.planning/phases/43.2-motion-and-static-group-stabilization-and-action-lifecycle-u/43.2-CONTEXT.md` (neutral deleted-gap cell treatment), `.planning/phases/43.1-intentional-gap-insert-use-specs-milestone-v0-9-0-new-phases/43.1-CONTEXT.md` (accepted-status copy precedent and product-reason mapper).

**Extension rule:** this contract adds a drag gesture, ghost preview, clamped blocked edge, neutral-gray gap preview, and status copy on top of the accepted Phase 43 rail contract. It changes no rail geometry, mode colors, selection colors, tooltip content, white endpoint cuts, strip height, or cell semantics from 43/43.1/43.2. Physical-key group drag behavior is a locked regression boundary (D-15) and is not respecified here.

## Geometry and Layering Contract

### Existing geometry is immutable

The workflow strip remains exactly **161px**; the physical-frame row remains **38px**; the visible rail remains exactly **3px** high at `y: 0`; each rail target remains **12px** high at `top: 0`; frame pitch remains **18px/frame**. The drag gesture, ghost, gap preview, and blocked edge add zero row, track, lane, or height. (43-UI-SPEC geometry contract, restated as the binding boundary.)

### G1 drag gesture geometry

- The drag origin is the existing 12px rail target. No new hit target, handle, grip affordance, or drag icon is added (43.2 D-23 / physics-paint-UI-spec-fidelity precedent: no noisy extra UI).
- A drag session starts only after the pointer travels more than **4px** horizontally from pointer-down (D-04). Below the threshold the gesture resolves as the existing plain click / Shift-click / Cmd-click selection; double-click Edit Group remains intact.
- During an active session the rail target holds pointer capture; cursor is `grabbing`. Before the threshold and on hover the cursor remains the existing `pointer`. `grab` is not used at rest, matching the accepted 43 contract that reserves grab cursors.
- **Escape** during a session cancels with zero state change, zero history entry, and immediate removal of ghost, gap preview, and blocked edge (D-04).
- Drop while clamped commits at the clamped position — the preview is the commit (D-05).

### G2 ghost rail geometry

- The ghost is a paint-only element with the same half-open interval geometry as the dragged rail segment: `left = proposedPlacement × 18px`, `width = max(1px, (effectiveEnd - proposedPlacement) × 18px)`, 3px high at `y: 0` of the physical-frame row, visible-window clipped exactly like the accepted rail.
- Ghost paint: the dragged Group's mode color (`#8B5CF6` Motion/Progressive, `#06B6D4` Static/Hold) at **55% opacity**, matching the existing rail busy-state opacity precedent. Effective 0f Groups ghost the existing 8×6px flag glyph at 55% opacity.
- The ghost never shows selection orange, white endpoint cuts, hover brightening, or lifecycle dots. It is a destination proposal, not a second accepted rail.
- The original rail stays fully visible in place at 100% opacity until commit (D-01). Nothing translates, dims, or hides before exact parent acceptance.
- Ghost z-order: above the transparent rail hit targets and the accepted 3px rail paint, below the rail focus ring and styled tooltip (inserts between existing layers 7 and 8 of the 43-UI-SPEC layer table). The ghost has `pointer-events: none`.

### G3 gap preview geometry

- The interval the Group would vacate, and any newly opened destination interval between the moved Group's first source key and its new predecessor, preview as ordinary empty cells: existing class `roto-fill-empty` (border `#3D454D`, background `#4D535A`) — byte-identical to the 43.2 deleted-Group-gap treatment (D-02). No distinct drag-gap visual, no ghost cells as a first-class surface, no diagonal hatching.
- The preview paints cells only; it changes no cell geometry, adds no DOM nodes beyond class application, and never shows a real-key diamond.
- Landing immediately adjacent to the predecessor previews no destination gap (D-10/D-12 mirrored in preview).

### G4 clamped blocked edge geometry

- When the ghost is clamped against a collision boundary (D-08: frame 0, physical capacity/parent end, any previous/next real key, any other Group interval including linked occurrences), a **2px wide × 12px high** vertical bar in `#FF6B6B` paints on the ghost's blocked edge — left edge when clamped against a left boundary, right edge when clamped against a right boundary.
- The bar spans the full 12px interaction band so the limit is readable at the rail scale, and uses the same 2px bar geometry idiom as the canonical white endpoint cuts, in the rail's existing unresolved red (D-03).
- The blocked edge is paint-only with `pointer-events: none`, shares the ghost's z-layer, and is absent whenever the ghost is not clamped.
- The ghost itself remains mode-colored at 55% opacity while clamped; the red bar is the sole blocked signal. The accepted rail's unresolved red treatment is unchanged.

### State precedence during drag

Highest visible state wins:

1. Clamped blocked edge `#FF6B6B` bar (ghost layer only)
2. Keyboard focus ring (accepted rail only)
3. Existing selection/hover/normal rail paint (accepted rail only)
4. Ghost mode paint at 55% opacity
5. Gap preview empty-cell fill (cell layer, beneath all rail paint)

The ghost never recolors the accepted rail; the accepted rail never inherits ghost, clamp, or gap state. State changes alter paint only, never geometry (43-UI-SPEC rule carried forward).

### Responsive contract at 1280×720 minimum

- Unchanged from 43-UI-SPEC: existing three-column layout, 161px strip, shared horizontal scroller.
- Ghost, blocked edge, and gap preview clip at the shared timeline viewport exactly like the accepted rail; horizontal scrolling during a drag follows existing timeline scroll behavior and recomputes the clamped destination from the live pointer position.
- No vertical scrolling, canvas-height change, or sidebar change is introduced.

**Accent reserved for:** existing normal/selected rail paint, the ghost rail's mode-colored paint, the selected-loop inspector accent, the contextual Edit button's active/hover affordance, and the existing linked-frame inset border/dot. The ghost introduces no new accent consumer; the clamped blocked edge uses error red, not accent.

### Drag-state paint table

| State | Exact treatment |
|-------|-----------------|
| Rest | existing rail paint unchanged (normal/hover/selected/truncated/unresolved per 43-UI-SPEC) |
| Pre-threshold pointer down | no paint change; cursor remains `pointer` |
| Active drag, unclamped | original rail unchanged at 100% opacity; ghost = mode color at 55% opacity at the proposed destination; vacated interval cells = `roto-fill-empty` |
| Active drag, clamped | ghost as above plus 2×12px `#FF6B6B` bar on the blocked edge; gap preview unchanged |
| Active drag, landing gap opens | destination-gap cells additionally preview `roto-fill-empty` |
| Commit (accepted) | ghost, blocked edge, and gap preview removed; accepted rail, cells, breaks, and status publish only from the exact parent acknowledgement |
| Escape cancel | all preview paint removed immediately; accepted state, selection, focus, and history untouched |
| Rejected (zero valid movement) | all preview paint removed; original rail and cells unchanged; status line publishes the mapped reason |

### Non-color cues

- The clamped limit is cued by the blocked-edge bar position (left vs right edge), not by color alone.
- Gap preview reuses the ordinary empty-cell fill that users already read as intentional empty space from 43.2 — a shape/fill cue, not a new hue.
- No drag state relies on color alone (43-UI-SPEC accessibility rule carried forward).

## Copywriting Contract

English product copy only. All drag copy flows through the single 43.1/43.2 product-reason mapper: one mapper owns disabled preflight, rejection, and acceptance copy; raw resolver diagnostics never enter product copy. Frame numbers use canonical physical app frames.

| Element | Copy |
|---------|------|
| Primary CTA | not applicable — the phase introduces a pointer drag gesture on the existing rail, not a button; no new control, menu item, or CTA label is added |
| No-space rejection (D-06) | `No empty space in that direction.` |
| Accepted move, no gap opened (D-07) | `Moved Group to frame {N}.` |
| Accepted move, gap opened (D-07) | `Moved Group to frame {N}.` then `Gap left at frames {A}–{B}.` (two status facts following the 43.1 accepted-status precedent `Inserted empty key at frame {N}. New interpolation segment started.`) |
| Empty state heading | not applicable — with zero Groups the rail is absent from the DOM (43 contract); no empty placeholder, label, or reserved space exists |
| Error state | covered by the rejection row; transport/stale-authority failures publish the existing mapped controller reason and leave every visible fact unchanged (D-18) |
| Destructive confirmation | not applicable — drag never deletes, stretches, splits, cuts, or changes Repeat/Infinity (D-19); no confirmation dialog is introduced |

Copy rules:

- `{N}` is the accepted destination `placementStart` frame. `{A}–{B}` is the vacated interval as an inclusive product frame range derived from canonical half-open ranges only at presentation time (43.2 presentation rule).
- The ghost, tooltip, and sidebar show no drag-progress copy (no live frame readout on the ghost); the destination is communicated spatially by the ghost and confirmed by the accepted status line.
- The existing rail tooltip content, selection copy, and sidebar facts are unchanged.

## Interaction Contract

### Gesture disambiguation (D-04)

| Input | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Pointer down on rail target | begin tracking; no paint change, no navigation, no drag session yet |
| Pointer moves ≤ 4px and releases | existing plain click behavior: rail selection, inspector update, physical selection cleared |
| Shift / Cmd(Ctrl) held on release | existing contiguous-range / toggle rail selection behavior |
| Double-click | existing Edit Group dialog open, exactly once |
| Pointer moves > 4px horizontally | drag session starts; pointer capture on the rail target; cursor `grabbing`; ghost appears at the clamped proposed destination; vacated interval previews as empty cells |
| Pointer moves during session | ghost recomputes the clamped destination; blocked-edge bar appears/disappears with clamp state; gap preview updates |
| Escape during session | cancel: zero state change, zero history, all preview paint removed; selection and focus unchanged |
| Release (drop) | commit request at the currently shown (possibly clamped) position; preview is the commit contract (D-05) |
| Release with zero valid movement in the dragged direction | rejection: preview removed, nothing mutates, status line publishes `No empty space in that direction.` (D-06) |

### Coexistence with physical-key group drag (D-15)

- Rail drag (whole Group, free-space clamp, gap/break recording) and physical-key group drag (rigid translation of a selected key set) are two gestures with separate contracts and separate intent paths. Physical group drag behavior, candidate kinds, preview view-model, and rejection plumbing are locked regression boundaries and remain byte-unchanged in behavior.
- The rail drag originates only on the 12px rail target band; the physical-cell drag originates only below `y: 12px` on physical cells. The existing top-band propagation stop is preserved; neither gesture can start the other.

### Accepted-only publication

- Ghost, blocked edge, and gap preview are presentation-only. The Group, keys, breaks, rail, selection, cursor, canvas, and history never present as moved before exact correlated parent acknowledgement.
- An accepted move is one atomic transaction (moved Group plus any break needed to preserve an opened gap) with one Undo/Redo; Undo restores the original placement and removes movement-created breaks together (D-09..D-12, domain boundary).
- After an accepted drag: the moved Group stays selected, the physical cursor stays where it is, no automatic navigation occurs, and the Studio canvas reconciles from the accepted document only when the current frame's content changed (D-17).
- A failed, rejected, or cancelled drag changes nothing and creates no history entry (D-18).

### Busy behavior

- During commit, the accepted rail remains visible in place; existing `aria-busy` and busy-opacity idioms from the 43 contract apply. No optimistic rail movement, width change, or status change occurs.
- A second drag session cannot start while a Group mutation is in flight; the existing controller busy gate owns this.

### Accessibility contract

- Rail target role, accessible name form, `aria-pressed`, tab order, Enter/Space/Escape keyboard behavior, and focus restoration are unchanged from 43-UI-SPEC.
- Status updates publish through the existing `aria-live="polite"` status line; rejections additionally use `role="alert"` (43-UI-SPEC rule).
- The ghost, blocked edge, and gap preview are `aria-hidden="true"` paint-only elements; they create no tab stop, no live-region churn during pointer movement, and no accessible-name mutation on the accepted rail.
- Focus visibility: the existing 2px `#F2F5F7` ring with 1px offset remains the sole focus treatment; starting a drag from keyboard is not supported (pointer-only gesture per the deferred keyboard-nudge note in 43.3-CONTEXT deferred list).
- Reduced motion: ghost, clamp, and gap-preview changes are immediate paint changes with no slide, tween, spring, shimmer, or pulse. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, no transition is added to any drag-touched element (43-UI-SPEC rule carried forward).

## Visual Acceptance Matrix (native UAT scope)

Native visual UAT remains the final oracle and runs from a fully restarted frozen-code parent/child session (43.2 D-39 carried forward). The matrix below is the visible-evidence core; the full 11-step product acceptance list lives in the activated phase prompt.

| # | User-visible check | Required evidence | Automatic rejection |
|---|--------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Threshold disambiguation | Click, Shift-click, Cmd-click, and double-click on the rail behave exactly as before; drag starts only past ~4px | Selection lost, dialog double-open, or drag starting on plain click |
| 2 | Ghost fidelity | Translucent mode-colored ghost follows the cursor at the proposed destination while the original rail stays fully visible in place | Original rail moving/dimming before commit, ghost showing selection orange or endpoint cuts |
| 3 | Gap preview language | Vacated and newly opened intervals preview in the same neutral-gray empty-cell treatment as 43.2 deleted Group gaps | Any distinct drag-gap color, hatch, badge, or ghost-cell surface |
| 4 | Clamp visibility | At a collision boundary the ghost clamps and the blocked edge shows the red bar before drop; drop commits the clamped preview | Overlap, crossing, reorder, or silent snap-back |
| 5 | No-space feedback | Dragging with zero free space publishes `No empty space in that direction.` with zero mutation | Modal, sound, silent snap-back, or any state change |
| 6 | Accepted status facts | Accepted move publishes `Moved Group to frame {N}.` plus `Gap left at frames {A}–{B}.` when a gap opened | Missing destination/gap facts or raw resolver diagnostics in copy |
| 7 | Escape cancellation | Escape mid-drag restores the exact pre-drag view with no history entry | Any residual ghost, gap paint, or history pollution |
| 8 | Post-commit stability | Moved Group stays selected, cursor unmoved, no automatic navigation; gap persists across Interpolation Off/On | Navigation jump, selection loss, or interpolation bridging the gap |
| 9 | Atomic Undo/Redo and persistence | One Undo restores the pre-drag document; one Redo reapplies it; save/reopen reproduces placement, gaps, and breaks | Split history entries or save/reopen divergence |
| 10 | Duplicated-placement independence | Dragging a duplicated placement moves only that placement and never retimes shared source keys | Any shared-source movement, clone, or materialization |
