# real-paint 01: Brush Footprint — Specification

**Created:** 2026-09-28
**Ambiguity score:** 0.06
**Requirements:** 6 locked
**Tranches covered:** 1 (footprint + erase + kill salt-and-pepper) and 2 (charge / dry brush)
**Supersedes the bristle pass of:** quick `260928-dh1` (see Background)

## Goal

Stroke edges change from salt-and-pepper pixel noise to contained, translucent bristle
streaks whose coverage follows brush density, orientation, velocity and pressure, and
whose paint load depletes along the stroke to produce dry-brush gaps — in both paint
and erase.

## Background

Today the live preview draws a flat filled ribbon (`fillFlat`) — clean but with zero
brush character — and the finalize raster adds `drawBristleTraces`
(`packages/efx-physic-paint/src/brush/paint.ts:70-137`, called at `:380`, `:422`,
`:483`): N ≈ `radius*0.5` thin strokes of width 0.4–1.8 px, alpha 0.015–0.06, dark
`×0.7` / light `×1.4`, sinusoidal wobble, and per-point skip on `Math.random()` plus a
paper-height cut at `h > 0.72`. The result reads as **salt-and-pepper concentrated on
the edges** — exactly where the brins skip most. Live captures (`test1`/`test2`
preview vs final) show the preview is already *more* pictorial than the final.

Quick `260928-dh1` (2026-09-28) replaced `Math.random()` with seeded draws and
de-hardcut `dryStep`, and shipped `util/traceSeed.ts` (FNV-1a over the mutation id,
draws keyed by `(strokeSeed, arcLength, drawKey)`). Its click-0 metric verdict is
**NOT MET** (`isolatedPx` 912 vs RED 889 — a wash; `edgeCliffs` flat at 21 703 vs
21 702) and native visual UAT is pending. The determinism infrastructure is sound and
**is reused**; the geometry it drives is what this spec replaces.

Gesture inputs are already carried by `PenPoint` (`p` pressure, `spd` velocity,
`tx` tilt/orientation), so the four named drivers need no new stroke-input pipeline.
`erase.ts` still uses `fillFlat` (`:75`, `:79`) — a clean cut beside a textured mark.

No UI is added (decision Q11): density follows brush radius; charge, granulation and
trace count are constants driven by the gesture alone. Nothing new is stored in the
document format.

## Requirements

1. **Structured bristle footprint**: the stroke raster is drawn as contained bristle
   streaks, not a flat fill plus noise dots.
   - Current: `fillFlat` ribbon + `drawBristleTraces` salt-and-pepper; brins are thin
     noisy strokes with random skip and a paper-height cut
   - Target: N bristle streaks laid along the ribbon, each a coherent directional
     streak with continuous low alpha; build-up by overlap, never by loud single pixels;
     trace width sub-2 px
   - Acceptance: at zoom 4, a stroke edge shows directional streaks aligned with the
     gesture; zero isolated pixels outside the ribbon footprint; `isolatedPx` metric at
     click-0 is 0 (not a wash against RED)

2. **Gesture-driven translucent edges**: edge translucency is a function of brush
   density, orientation, velocity and pressure.
   - Current: opacity is a constant per brin (`0.015 + Math.random()*0.06`) and pressure
     only modulates width (`pMod` at `:129-130`)
   - Target: coverage density follows brush density (radius-derived), streak direction
     follows gesture orientation, velocity leaves more gaps and a thinner deposit,
     pressure lightens and narrows the deposit
   - Acceptance: scripted capture matrix (the dh1 harness) shows light pressure
     measurably lighter than heavy pressure in body alpha mass, and fast stroke
     measurably more depleted than slow stroke

3. **Per-stroke dry-brush charge**: paint load depletes along the stroke.
   - Current: no load model — the stroke is equally full from start to end
   - Target: load starts full at stroke start and depletes with arc length, depletion
     accelerated by velocity and pressure; the stroke dries out and skips at the end of
     its run. No inter-stroke state (fresh stroke = fresh load), no reload gesture
   - Acceptance: a long scripted stroke visibly dries out toward its end; replaying the
     identical stroke reproduces the identical gap pattern (seeded)

4. **Erase uses the same footprint family**: erase marks match the painted mark's
     character.
   - Current: `erase.ts` uses `fillFlat` — a hard cut beside a textured stroke
   - Target: erase deposits the same bristle footprint and the same charge model with a
     **different seed** (so it never aligns with the erased mark); no granulation, no
     pigment mixing on erase
   - Acceptance: an erased patch on a textured stroke shows structured translucent edges,
     not a rectangular or hard-edged cut

5. **Deterministic, seeded traces**: all trace geometry is a pure function of
   `(stroke seed, arc length, parameters)`.
   - Current: `traceSeed.ts` exists and is seeded, but `drawBristleTraces` geometry is
     still being regenerated with the noise character this spec replaces
   - Target: all footprint and charge randomness draws from `traceSeed.ts`
     (`hashMutationId` + arc-length-keyed draws); no `Math.random()` anywhere in the
     deposit path; no non-deterministic source added
   - Acceptance: same stroke id + arc length produces byte-identical bristle geometry
     across two runs; a different stroke id produces different traces (extend
     `paint.bristleSeed.test.ts`)

6. **One routine, two quality tiers**: live and finalize share the footprint routine.
   - Current: live draws `fillFlat`, finalize draws `fillFlat` + `drawBristleTraces` —
     two different looks from two different paths
   - Target: a single `drawBristleFootprint(curve, params, tier)` where `tier` selects
     `live` (~¼ of the brins) or `final` (full N ≈ radius). Per DiVerdi §4.1, bristle
     **thickness scales up as count scales down** so both tiers have the same coverage
     and silhouette — the finalize then refines the live mark and never rewrites it
   - Acceptance: live and finalize of the same stroke show the same silhouette and the
     same gap positions; finalize only adds detail. No second footprint implementation
     exists in the tree

## Boundaries

**In scope:**
- `packages/efx-physic-paint/src/brush/paint.ts` — replace `drawBristleTraces` geometry
  with the structured footprint routine
- `packages/efx-physic-paint/src/brush/erase.ts` — route erase through the same routine
- Charge / dry-brush load model at deposit time
- Seeded draws through `util/traceSeed.ts`
- Quality tier parameter (live ¼ / final full)
- Extension of the dh1 capture harness acceptance rows to the look (directional streaks,
  not only metric counts)

**Out of scope:**
- Pigment granulation — that is spec 02 (a finish, not geometry)
- Pigment mixing (`kmerp`) — that is spec 03
- Paper tooth / `paperPass` — unchanged, spec 04
- Any new UI control — explicitly rejected (Q11)
- Changes to the stroke-input pipeline — `PenPoint` already carries the four drivers
- Physical bristle simulation (bending, collision) — rejected in favour of a statistical
  footprint (Q3): too costly for a roto/anim tool that must keep the gesture real-time
- Captured brush stamps (bitmap footprints) — rejected (Q3): frozen look, asset-bound

## Constraints

- **Real-time gesture budget.** The product law is *"le geste prioritaire — la peinture
  s'affiche en temps réel, la physique s'applique après sur des workers, le rendu se met
  en pause quand je peins"*. Concretely the live redraw must stay inside one frame at
  60 fps (~16 ms) and feel instant. Only the live tier may add cost to the gesture —
  granulation and mixing are finalize-time. A tighter provisional figure (~2× the current
  `fillFlat` ribbon) was floated in an earlier draft of this spec; it is **not** a project
  law and must not be treated as one. The real number comes from the cost spike
  (`05-research-notes.md` §6.6) measured in the live WKWebView on an M3.
- **Geometry only, no per-pixel passes.** The footprint is ribbon/polygon/`ctx.stroke`
  draws. `getImageData` + per-pixel loops are forbidden in the stroke raster — they were
  deleted in 260925-dso for latency and must not return.
- **Reuse `traceSeed.ts`.** FNV-1a over the mutation id, arc-length-keyed draws. No new
  seeding scheme, no RNG module.
- **Existing deposit laws stay green at their existing bounds:** `PIN 0`/`0b` body
  opacity, envelope `W_visible ≤ 8`, texture `d(b) ≥ 1`, `DEPOSIT_KEEP_TIER = 70`,
  `DRY_ALPHA_THRESHOLD = 1`, W1–W7, texture-at-Spread-80.
- **No new packages.** `package.json` and `pnpm-lock.yaml` stay empty diffs.
- **Nothing new in the document format** — no per-stroke params stored.
- **Stack boundary — engine only.** All new code is pure TypeScript in
  `packages/efx-physic-paint`, drawn with Canvas 2D. The stroke raster must never import
  Preact/React — it is a render engine, not UI.
- **Stack boundary — async discipline.** Any stateful async path (stroke finalize, physics
  hand-off) follows `efx-async-orchestration` (XState v6 + Effect v4) — no hand-rolled FSM out
  of timers and boolean flags. `efx-preact-reactivity` is out of scope here (no UI, Q11).
- **Open finding F1** (`05-research-notes.md` §4) — three distinct "dry" mechanisms exist and
  must not be conflated: DiVerdi's **thin bristles** (§4.1, a look), DiVerdi's **reservoir +
  pickup depletion** (§4.2, what Q9 simplifies), and Curtis's **paper-height threshold** (§4.7).
  Decide whether to AND the third in before implementing.
- **Open cost decision** (`05-research-notes.md` §6.6) — whether this footprint stays a
  statistical model or grows toward DiVerdi's bristle solver is settled by a **measured spike**,
  not by arithmetic. DiVerdi's own timings are 2008-era and scale ~25–50× on Apple Silicon.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] At zoom 4, stroke edges show directional translucent bristle streaks aligned with
      the gesture — no isolated pixels, no salt-and-pepper
- [ ] dh1 harness click-0 `isolatedPx == 0` (not a wash vs RED) and `edgeCliffs` at or
      near 0
- [ ] Light pressure renders measurably lighter body alpha mass than heavy pressure
- [ ] Fast stroke renders measurably more depleted than slow stroke
- [ ] A long stroke visibly dries out toward its end (dry-brush gaps appear)
- [ ] Replaying the identical stroke reproduces the identical gap and trace pattern
- [ ] A different stroke id produces a different trace pattern
- [ ] Erased patches show structured translucent edges, not a hard cut
- [ ] Live and finalize of the same stroke share silhouette and gap positions
- [ ] `Math.random()` has zero occurrences in the deposit path
- [ ] Invariant battery green at existing bounds: PIN 0/0b, envelope ≤ 8, texture
      d(b) ≥ 1, `DEPOSIT_KEEP_TIER = 70`, `DRY_ALPHA_THRESHOLD = 1`, W1–W7
- [ ] `package.json` / `pnpm-lock.yaml` diffs empty
