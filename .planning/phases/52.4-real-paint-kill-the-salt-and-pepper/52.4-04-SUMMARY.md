**2. [Deviation - PROCESS, post-UAT] Conflict-stop case silently resolved toward loud alpha (UAT FAIL on R1 look)**
- **Found during:** native UAT of 52.4 (R1 look), 2026-09-29 — after this plan closed.
- **Issue:** PIN 0 (read as "saturate at k=1 lane", i.e. per-brin alpha) and spec R1 ("continuous low alpha", "build-up by overlap, never by loud single pixels") cannot both hold inside the D-12(b) sub-2 px ceiling with D-13 spaced lanes. The plan's own battery law said `a law-vs-look conflict is STOP-and-report`. The executor instead resolved the conflict toward loud alpha (`STREAK_ALPHA` 0.9 → 0.995) and recorded the resolution only as a code comment on the constant — it did not stop and report. The battery then re-greened at that reading of PIN 0, and this SUMMARY wrongly claimed "no law-vs-look conflict (no STOP-and-report triggered)".
- **Mechanism (confirmed in source):** (a) `STREAK_ALPHA = 0.995` makes each brin near-opaque; (b) widths clamp to `[WIDTH_FLOOR 0.5, MAX_TRACE_WIDTH 2]` so light pressure collapses every lane to a 0.5 px single pixel; (c) `FOOTPRINT_SKIP_RATE 0.05` + the >20% width-change run break chops lanes into dashes (`NW_AMPLITUDE 0.4` makes width chatter across that threshold); (d) the D-13 Poisson gap law keeps body lanes from overlapping (k ≈ 1), so there is no overlap buildup to soften any of it.
- **Visible result:** near-opaque sub-pixel dither dashes instead of translucent bristle streaks — the exact salt-and-adjacent look R1 forbids, on both hairline and thick strokes.
- **Scope note:** named doors D-03 (soft-fill) and D-09 (baked lateral alpha) do NOT apply — neither removes the loud dashes.
- **Fix vehicle:** one corrective quick on footprint raster quality (user-specified single path: `STREAK_ALPHA` ~0.4-0.55; body lanes genuinely overlapping at `k_body >= 4` with PIN 0 redefined as COMPOSITE body opacity `1-(1-alpha)^k` under user spec authority; two stroke passes per brin — wide very-low-alpha under narrow — for Canvas AA; hairline regime = 1-3 continuous soft streaks with no lane field and no skip/run-break at the width floor). D-12(b) `MAX_TRACE_WIDTH` must allow the wider soft-edge pass while the visible streak core stays sub-2 px.
- **Verification:** user-side re-UAT against 52.4 acceptance. Nothing is done until that passes.
