<objective>
Ship three textured tips on every install, baked from the user-supplied source art into compile-time constants and painted through the same mapping as every other tip.

Purpose: success criterion 3 must be true with zero imports, and TIP-13 makes "compile-time constants" literal. The bake is an authoring step whose output is committed; runtime never sees a JPEG, a generator or a file path (D-06).
Output: `builtinTipMasks.generated.ts` (three base64 masks), `builtinTips.ts` (three frozen tip records + decoded masks), and a registry that seeds and resets to them.

`builtinTips.ts` is the SINGLE source of the three texture tip records for the rest of the phase: `BUILTIN_TIPS` (ids, labels, neutral multipliers) is what the catalog store, the library sections and the catalog payload import — no later plan re-declares, renames or copies the three ids.
</objective>
