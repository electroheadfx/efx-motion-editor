**Always-visible #F5A623 diamonds per real Roto key on physic-paint FX rows of the main timeline — one FxTrackLayout field, one frameMap population line, one clipped canvas draw pass, pure-function geometry tested including the nonzero-inFrame case.**

## Accomplishments

- New `FxTrackLayout.rotoKeyFrames?: number[]` field (app/src/types/timeline.ts) — layer-local physical appFrames of real Roto keys (C-04), documented beside `playScriptMarkers` without reviving the dormant Play path.
- `fxTrackLayouts` computed (app/src/lib/frameMap.ts) populates `rotoKeyFrames` only when `primaryLayer?.type === 'physic-paint'`, mapping `physicPaintStore.getRotoRealKeyRecords(getLayerId(primaryLayer))` to `record.appFrame`; all other layer types get `undefined`. Real-keys-only semantics (D-07) are structural — the store port never returns generated interiors.
- New exported pure `getPhysicPaintRotoKeyMarkerGeometry({ appFrame, inFrame, frameWidth, scrollX })` returning `{ x: (inFrame + appFrame) * frameWidth - scrollX + TRACK_HEADER_WIDTH }`, mirroring the `drawKeyframeDiamonds` FX coordinate math so nonzero-inFrame layers place diamonds correctly.
- New private `drawRotoKeyMarkers` in TimelineRenderer: clips to the FX bar roundRect (same pattern as `drawPhysicPaintPlayScriptMarkers`), per-marker viewport culling, diamond path (top/right/bottom/left) at half-extent 9 matching the keyframe icon size, single `#F5A623` fill, no stroke, no shadow, no hit-test or interaction surface (D-08/D-09). Invoked from the `drawFxTrack` physic-paint branch gated on `fxTrack.rotoKeyFrames?.length` — always visible, never selection-gated (D-06).
- Reactivity proven against the real store + computed path: replacing physical records via `replaceRotoPhysicalRecords` recomputes `rotoKeyFrames` through the existing `physicPaintVersion` read at the top of `fxTrackLayouts` — no new subscription or invalidation machinery.
