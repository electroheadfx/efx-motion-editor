## Decisions Made

- Deleted identities lose their owned child entries, while surviving identities carry their complete child state to the accepted direct appFrame; unowned or colliding entries fail the rebuild closed.
- Successful physical document installation advances visual invalidation without marking project content dirty; accepted content mutations mark dirty once, while stale/rejected/no-change outcomes do not publish.
- Real and generated renderer cache identity includes the accepted physical content revision and stable ownership metadata rather than a frame/data-length approximation.
- Export remains a client of PreviewRenderer and does not import the Roto store or derive a second timing/rendering model.
- The user-approved sequencing keeps the exact legacy cache module dormant and production-unimported until Plan 13 deletes it together with `rotoCacheTransactions.test.ts` after byte-exact Plan 12 approval and valid assertion transfer.
