## Phase Goal

**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>
Build the single acknowledged physical-edit coordinator per D-01, D-03, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-25, and D-26. It serializes pending work, revalidates launch/layer/revision before staging and settlement, captures immutable complete snapshots, stages one complete physical proposal once, publishes one closed payload once, matches one exact acknowledgement, and restores fail-closed state for every unsuccessful or cancelled outcome.

Purpose: Replace the accepted move-only lifecycle with one operation-agnostic physical transaction boundary that later Insert, Delete, Drag, Force Spacing, Undo, and Redo plans can call without creating independent reliability protocols. This plan establishes acknowledgement correctness and rollback ownership; Plan 05 owns the generic accepted-only history cursor, and Plans 06-08 own operation delivery semantics.

Output: A deterministic physical revision/envelope, parent complete-map authority, one Preact-native coordinator, generalized bridge settlement, complete snapshot/rebuild ports, Studio composition wiring, and concise status-versus-LOG routing.
</objective>
