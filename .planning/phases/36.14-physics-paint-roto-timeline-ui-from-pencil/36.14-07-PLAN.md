## Phase Goal

**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>
Deliver single-key ripple Drag per D-01, D-02, D-03, D-07, D-09, D-11, D-12, D-21, D-22, D-23, and D-24 as complete versioned cut-and-insert proposals for empty/generated whole-cell destinations and occupied before/after identity boundaries. Preserve the native-approved pointer lifecycle while making the resolver's one immutable complete proposal the only preview and commit authority, submitting it through the shared coordinator for latest-state revalidation, and preventing every overwrite, no-op, stale, malformed, partial, or over-capacity proposal from publication.

Purpose: Let users reposition one real Roto key with predictable physical ripple behavior while seeing the exact complete result before release and retaining identity-owned paint through acknowledgement, rollback, selection, focus, and history.

Output: Versioned Drag preparation/commit actions, identity-based pointer targeting, occupied boundary intent, complete proposal-driven preview, exact coordinator submission, accepted focus/history handoff, cancellation restoration, and removal of the active legacy frame-pair move route.
</objective>
