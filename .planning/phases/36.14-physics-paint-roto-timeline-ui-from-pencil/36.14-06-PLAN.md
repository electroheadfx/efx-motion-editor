## Phase Goal

**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>
Deliver ripple Insert Frame and Delete Frame through the physical resolver, acknowledged coordinator, and accepted-only history established by Plans 02, 04, and 05 per D-01, D-03, D-05, D-06, D-09, D-11, and D-12. Preserve payload ownership by stable identity, reject every invalid or over-capacity complete map before optimistic publication, make survivor selection deterministic, and route visible buttons plus Delete/Backspace through shared semantic actions without retaining an active legacy transaction route.

Purpose: Give users trustworthy physical timeline insertion and deletion whose visible result, persisted result, selection, and Undo/Redo command all describe the same accepted complete state.

Output: Shared Preact-native Insert/Delete actions, Studio composition wiring, physical action availability, one toolbar route per action, one guarded Delete/Backspace route, and a runtime cutover away from the former session-owned Insert/Delete callbacks.
</objective>
