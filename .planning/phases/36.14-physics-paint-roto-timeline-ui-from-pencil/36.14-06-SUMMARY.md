# Phase 36.14 Plan 06: Ripple Insert/Delete through resolver, coordinator, and accepted history

**Identity-preserving ripple Insert Frame, ripple Delete Frame with deterministic survivor selection, one stable physical timeline action bundle, toolbar/keyboard intent routing, and deleted legacy key transactions — Insert/Delete own no arithmetic, staging, publication, or history append**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~75 min
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 9 (modified 8, deleted 1)

## Accomplishments
- Extended `useRotoTimelineActions` with one stable `RotoPhysicalTimelineActionBundle` owning `insertRotoFrame`, `deleteRotoFrame`, `canInsertFrame`, `canDeleteFrame`, `insertDisabledReason`, `deleteDisabledReason`, and `pendingOperationId`; one private `runPhysicalAction` runner collects coherent authoritative state, invokes `resolvePhysicPaintRotoPhysicalEdit`, and submits the immutable complete proposal to `executePhysicalEdit`.
- Insert routes `insert-slot` through the pure resolver, preserves the complete identity set, moves the selected-and-later suffix right once, leaves an empty physical slot at the selected key's former frame, and selects the same stable identity at its new frame; Delete routes `delete-key` through the pure resolver, removes exactly the selected identity, shifts every later survivor left once, leaves earlier survivors fixed, and selects the resolver-chosen successor/previous/null.
- Availability is derived directly from current selected stable identity, launch readiness, and the coordinator's one pending Signal; no copied hook state, no second busy mirror, no effect-driven action synchronization.
- Migrated retained Duplicate/Paste/Save helpers (`claimRotoSelectedFrame`, `saveRotoRealKeyTransaction`, `updateRotoInterpolationSettingsTransaction`) plus their types from `rotoKeyTransactions.ts` to `physicsPaintRotoKeyController.ts` alongside its existing legacy source/display helpers; deleted `rotoKeyTransactions.ts` without a forwarding export.
- Removed `insert`/`delete` cases from `buildRotoKeyUtilityTransaction`, removed `'insert' | 'delete'` from `RotoKeyUtilityOperation`, and removed `insertBlankKey`/`deleteKey` from `useRotoKeyUtilities` and `physicsPaintRotoSession` (Rule 3 blocking fix).
- Wired visible Insert/Delete buttons in `PhysicsPaintWorkflowStrip` directly to `rotoPhysicalActions.insertRotoFrame`/`deleteRotoFrame`; Insert button aria-label now describes inserting an empty frame before the selected real key; button availability derives from physical `canInsertFrame`/`canDeleteFrame` signals with concise disabled reasons from the bundle.
- Wired the Studio keyboard `deleteRotoKey` action to the exact same `rotoPhysicalActions.deleteRotoFrame` reference passed to the Workflow Strip; preserved unmodified Delete/Backspace equivalence, non-repeat behavior, modifier rejection, editable-control/contenteditable suppression, dialog/modal suppression, and the current-cell exception.
- Kept `app/src/lib/shortcuts.ts` unchanged: its global editor shortcuts continue to honor `isPaintEditMode()` for conflicting keys and its normal editor deletion hierarchy remains separate from the focused Physics Paint Studio action.
