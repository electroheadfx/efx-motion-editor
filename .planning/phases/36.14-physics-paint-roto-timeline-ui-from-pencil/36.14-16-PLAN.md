<edge_case_coverage>
- Pointer release below threshold: disarm and preserve normal click behavior; no proposal commit or click suppression.
- Pointer release after threshold with no valid retained proposal: suppress the drag's synthetic click, cancel, and publish nothing.
- Release target differs from retained visible preview: cancel rather than preparing and committing an unseen target.
- Self/no-change target: consume resolver rejection/no-op metadata; show no valid affordance and publish nothing.
- Invalid or stale proposal identity/version: do not commit, settle, focus, or scroll.
- Unknown occupied target keyId: consume resolver rejection; never infer an insertion boundary locally.
- Empty target: render whole-cell feedback on the supplied empty proposal cell.
- Generated target: retain generated fill/label while whole-cell feedback overlays it; do not promote it to a real key.
- Occupied target: preserve the real key and render only the supplied before/after caret; never remove or replace the target.
- Stationary pointer during edge scroll: after actual scroll movement, re-hit-test and render the newly supplied complete proposal.
- Edge scroll at either bound: stop semantic refresh when scrollLeft cannot change; keep one rAF lifecycle.
- Window blur before threshold: cancel the armed session without capture, publication, or delayed drag.
- Window blur after capture: abort, release capture if held, cancel rAF/listeners, clear transient presentation, and suppress the drag click.
- `lostpointercapture` after Escape, blur, pointercancel, or unmount cleanup: inactive session makes the repeated event a no-op.
- Pointer capture throws or is unavailable after threshold: abort without publication and retain one-shot click suppression for the actual drag.
- Pending operation at pointerdown: do not arm, capture, prepare, or replace the existing proposal/operation.
- Matching acceptance after edge scroll changed the viewport: focus by keyId, then reveal only the final accepted cell's remaining clipped delta.
- Accepted final cell fully visible: focus with preventScroll and leave scrollLeft unchanged.
- Accepted final cell clipped left: scroll left by the exact clipped amount only.
- Accepted final cell clipped right: scroll right by the exact clipped amount only.
- Rejection while committing proposal is visible: retain it until the matching rejection state arrives, then restore authoritative selection and prior focus without local rollback.
- Source/prior identity absent after launch replacement: focus the stable timeline container without selecting a replacement identity.
- Terminal result belongs to another proposal, version, operation, or launch: ignore it for proposal clearing, focus, and scrolling.
- Actual drag later rejected or cancelled: suppress its synthetic click exactly once.
- Cancellation before threshold: do not create post-drag suppression because no drag started.
- Repeated cleanup events: no duplicate commit, rollback request, focus settlement, capture release failure, listener leak, or stale rAF callback.
</edge_case_coverage>
