## Phase Goal

**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>
Reconstruct persistence, reopen hydration, bridge payloads/revisions, editable and live caches, dirty state, and project equality from immutable physical records and complete identity maps while preserving accepted visual-version publication per D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04, D-09, D-10, D-11, and D-12.

Purpose: Make accepted physical edits durable and reopenable without identity drift, cache drift, stale pixel publication, partial hydration, or a second equality/timing authority. This plan closes the persistence and mutable-ownership boundary after Force Spacing; Plan 10 owns playback, onion/reference traversal, preview, export, missing/background rendering, and timeline-length consumer cutover.

Output: Strict persisted physical records, atomic project reopen, revisioned launch/bridge reconstruction, one canonical physical equality path, identity-based cache/editable ownership rebuild, stale-safe live-pixel publication, and monotonic accepted visual invalidation.
</objective>
