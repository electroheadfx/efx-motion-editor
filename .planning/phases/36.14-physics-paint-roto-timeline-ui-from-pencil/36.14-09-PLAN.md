**As a** stop-motion animator, **I want to** have every real Roto key, generated in-between, timeline edit, persisted frame, and rendered output use one deterministic physical-frame authority, followed by a polished timeline UI that exposes the approved behavior without duplicating business logic, **so that** editing, reopening, previewing, and exporting the same Roto work cannot disagree about frame identity.

<objective>

Purpose: Make accepted physical edits durable and reopenable without identity drift, partial hydration, stale bridge revision, or a second equality/timing authority. Cache/live-pixel ownership, dirty-state semantics, visual invalidation, and downstream rendering remain bounded to Plan 10.

Output: Strict persisted physical records, atomic project save/open, revisioned launch/bridge reconstruction, one canonical physical equality/encoding path, and deletion of obsolete persistence-specific wrappers.
</objective>
