### Gaps Summary

The three gap-closure plans landed and are test-green (302/302 across the five touched suites), and 43.6-07 (batch undo/redo) plus 43.6-09 (solo tint + armed gate) fully achieve their must-haves — 43.6-09 additionally passed blocking native UAT. However, both code-review warnings are CONFIRMED against the actual source:

1. **WR-01 (cross-type seed drop):** the 43.6-08 seed bridge carries a plain-selected rail into the set only when the modifier-clicked target is of the SAME rail type. Plain-select Loop A then Cmd+click Key Rail B (or the reverse) silently drops A — half of the UI-SPEC M1 contract and a direct hit on the phase goal's "explicit cross-type rail SET" language. One-line-derivation fix in each of the two Studio handlers.
2. **WR-02 (double-click gate swallows modifier clicks):** on Loop Clip rails, a modifier click landing within the pending-single-click (250ms) or fast-double-click (220ms) window is routed to the open-editor branch — committing 'plain' and opening the Edit Group editor instead of the set membership. This partially undermines the G-43.6-8 closure and the rapid plain->Shift anchoring flow of G-43.6-1. One-condition fix (`gesture === 'plain' &&`) at the gate.

Both gaps share a theme: the 43.6-08 fixes cover the same-type / normal-timing paths but miss the cross-type and inside-the-window paths. A single focused closure plan covering both handlers' seed derivation and the Loop Clip gate condition, plus two regression tests, closes both. Native UAT for G-43.6-1/-3/-4/-8 should be scheduled after that closure.
