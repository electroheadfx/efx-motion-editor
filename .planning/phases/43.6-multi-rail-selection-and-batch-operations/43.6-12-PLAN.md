<objective>
Close the PRIMARY defect of G-43.6-2: multi-rail Delete Rails cannot be undone/redone
(D-06, D-23), while single-rail delete-key-rail can.

Root cause (line-verified): because 'delete-rails' is in GROUP_LIFECYCLE_OPERATION_KINDS
(physicPaintBridge.ts:1039-1047), the bridge's childBeforeAuthority branch records the
POST-delete proposal selection the forward payload ships (payload.selectedKeyId =
'source-B' after deleting the rail holding pre-op selection 'ordinary') as the 'before'
snapshot (:1644-1649). The coordinator's history entry.before, by contrast, carries the
TRUE pre-op selection (captureSnapshot reads lastAcceptedSelectionRef, the last
parent-accepted selection). On undo the history hook submits entry.before.selectedKeyId
('ordinary'); the bridge computes the proposedTargetSnapshot from the replay payload
('ordinary') and compares it against the recorded original.before ('source-B') — the
replay-target equality check (:1583-1606) rejects and undo silently no-ops. Single-rail
delete-key-rail is not in GROUP_LIFECYCLE_OPERATION_KINDS, so it records the true pre-op
selection and undoes correctly — the single/multi asymmetry.

Purpose: record the true pre-operation selection/cursor from currentDocument for
delete-rails before snapshots, so the undo replay payload always matches and one Undo
restores every rail/key/break AND the exact pre-delete selection set.

Output: one scoped production change in physicPaintBridge.ts (a
beforeSelectionFromPayload guard that excludes 'delete-rails' from payload-derived
selection), a REAL-bridge forward+undo+redo regression test in physicPaintBridge.test.ts,
and a history-seam round-trip pin in useRotoPhysicalEditHistory.test.ts. No coordinator,
store, or Studio change in this plan (the secondary G-43.6-2 defect is plan 43.6-13).
</objective>
