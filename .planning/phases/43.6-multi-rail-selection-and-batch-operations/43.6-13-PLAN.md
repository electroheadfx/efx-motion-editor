<objective>
Close the SECONDARY defect of G-43.6-2: the transient 'all deletes blocked until relaunch' the
user reported on the Delete-with-multi-select and Delete-with-undo test.

Root cause (line-verified): `releasePhysicalEditRecoveryLease`
(useRotoPhysicalEditCoordinator.ts:1260-1265) has NO production caller. When a deferred
physical publication fails (e.g. the failFirstLoopReplace path), `finalizeFailed(..., {
transferLeaseToRecovery: true })` (:1120-1131) flips the exclusive lease to recovery
ownership and sets `recoveryLeaseSignal`. Because nothing ever releases it, every later
`executePhysicalEdit` hits the `recoveryLeaseSignal.peek()` guard (:1269-1277) and returns
false with PHYSICAL_EDIT_SERIALIZE_MESSAGE — `isRotoPhysicalOperationAvailable` stays false
(physicPaintStore.ts:1480-1485) so `mutationLocked` stays true (PhysicsPaintStudio.tsx:627-642)
and every Delete/Backspace silently dies until the app relaunch clears the lease map
(physicPaintStore.ts:1339-1340).

Purpose: wire the orphaned release as a best-effort self-heal at the top of
executePhysicalEdit — the ONLY production caller — so a stale recovery lease clears before
the serialize guard, restoring edit availability without weakening the real concurrency
authority (the store's validateRotoPhysicalOperationLease).

Output: a guarded best-effort `releasePhysicalEditRecoveryLease()` call inserted at the top
of executePhysicalEdit (before the guard), the callback added to that useCallback's deps, and
a coordinator-level regression test proving a recovery lease left by an accepted-then-failed
Group publication does not block the next edit. No store, bridge, Studio, or history change
in this plan (the PRIMARY G-43.6-2 defect is plan 43.6-12).
</objective>
