<objective>
Add a session-only wireframe view that suspends the bake and shows every stroke's construction geometry,
built as a generalisation of the shipped Draft mode rather than a second suppression path.

Purpose: VEC-05. The whole phase assumes users can see what a vector path is doing; without wireframe,
per-vertex width and anchor shape are invisible. The risk is architectural — a second way to suppress
the engine would multiply every gate; extending Draft keeps exactly one.

Output: the latch, the rail toggle, the Studio gates with a single-flight off transition, the wireframe
drawing law for both stroke families, and the tests that pin the gates and the dirty-off law.
</objective>
