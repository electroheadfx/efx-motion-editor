<objective>
Satisfy REL-02 for the new vector fields: declare the anchor and vector-path shapes in both
TypeScript and Rust, pinned by the field contract test, and prove the opaque engine slot carries
them through a save/load cycle untouched.

Purpose: the project's settled law is that a `.mce` field existing in one language only is a defect
the contract test catches. The vector fields must not become the first exception — and because the
engine slot is opaque on the wire, the risk is not corruption but silent non-declaration: the field
works today and drops out of the contract the first time someone regenerates the models.

Output: two `Mce*` members in both languages, the contract rows that pin them, and the lossless
envelope round trip.
</objective>
