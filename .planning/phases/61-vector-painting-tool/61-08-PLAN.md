<objective>
Put the tool where the user can reach it: a fifth, visually separated rail entry with its own session
state, its own shortcut, and a mutual-exclusion law that keeps it out of the four paint tools and out
of the document entirely.

Purpose: every later plan needs `vectorPenActive` and `pathEditMutationId` to exist before drawing or
editing can be gated. C-01 is explicit that the real tool row is `WATERCOLOR_RAIL_TOOLS` in
`PhysicsPaintToolRail.tsx` and that `AquarelleToolRow.tsx` is dead — editing the dead file would pass
every test in this plan and ship nothing.

Output: `vectorPathSession.ts` with its exclusion matrix, the rail entry and separator, the bare-V
keyboard branch, and the three test files that pin them.
</objective>
