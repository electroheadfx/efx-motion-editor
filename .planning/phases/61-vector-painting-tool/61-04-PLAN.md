<objective>
Give the engine a width channel that can exceed the current footprint ceiling, fed per sample from
the sparse anchor factors, and prove on real pixels that a 1.8x anchor deposits a 1.8x-wide stroke.

Purpose: C-03 is the phase's sharpest trap. `scriptScale` is assigned with `Math.min(1, maxRadius /
radius(...))` at `Engine.ts:844`, so any plan that routes per-vertex width through it silently caps
at 1.0 and passes every unit test while the feature does not work. This plan adds a separate factor,
carries it on the sample rather than in engine state, and closes with a measurement rather than a
claim.

Output: the engine width channel, the adapter's derivation of per-sample factors from the sparse
authority, the adapter pins, and a probe scenario that measures the ratio end-to-end.
</objective>
