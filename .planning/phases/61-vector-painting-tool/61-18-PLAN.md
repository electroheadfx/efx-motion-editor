<objective>
Close the wireframe capture hole: with the construction view on, nothing may be captured, reset, re-baked
or persisted, every route that could start a bake is refused, and every refusal says why.

Purpose: G-7, and the one prohibition 61-13 left unresolved — "MUST NOT bake or capture a frame while
wireframe is on". The wireframe forces Draft ON at entry, but Draft could still be switched off while the
latch stayed on: entering Select performs a Draft reset on its entry edge, and the workflow strip's Draft
control calls the settle directly. Either route drops `draftMode` below the capture gate
(`!draftMode.peek()` at the capture seam) and lets a wireframe frame be captured, or lets the settle
reset and re-apply the sheet behind the construction view.

Output: the Draft-OFF refusal in the one shared edition module, the settle guard in the Studio, the rail's
Select gate with its reason, and the pins that keep the shipped pen-and-latch behaviour exactly as it is.
</objective>

dependency is also semantic — 61-15 introduces the vector commit route that reads `wireframeMode.peek()`
to defer, and this plan closes the remaining Draft-OFF and settle routes around it.

Project law for this plan: D-16 — wireframe GENERALISES Draft; it is never a second engine-suppression
path. The guard therefore belongs in `setDraftMode` (the one setter) and in the one settle, never in a
third latch. `efx-preact-reactivity`: the rail reads the latch in its render body (it is the narrow
subscriber); the Studio reads it with `.peek()` inside handlers only. `efx-async-orchestration`: the
settle's refusal is a bounded terminal state — publish one fixed reason and resolve `false`, never a
rejected promise and never a retry (§5 failures are data); the OFF transition's single settle stays the
one recompute.
