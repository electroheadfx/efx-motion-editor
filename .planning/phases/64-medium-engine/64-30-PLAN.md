<objective>
Close IN-02 by making the sheet cache's park and restore sides share one definition of a cacheable script, and record IN-06 as a written deferral.

The sheet cache remembers a frame's sheet under the digest of its event list, and restores it when the same digest returns. `restoreCachedSheet` refuses an empty or absent event list, because there is nothing to identify. `rememberSheet` guards only on the event list being truthy, so an empty array is parked under a valid digest and can never be taken. Nothing is visibly wrong today — an empty script paints nothing — but the asymmetry means the cache's advertised invariant ("what I remember, I can take") is false, and a later change to the digest or the restore guard would turn the parked entry into a stale hit.

Purpose: the IC-02 finding is small, and its value is entirely in removing an asymmetry before it becomes load-bearing. The predicate belongs next to the digest: both answer questions about a script, and the module that owns "what is this script" should own "is there a script".

Output: an exported `hasCacheableScript` predicate with a truth-table gate, both remember sites and the restore site wired to it, contract pins, and an IN-06 deferral section in the phase's deferred-items file.
</objective>
