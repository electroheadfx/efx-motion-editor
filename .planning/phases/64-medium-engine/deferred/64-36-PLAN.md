<objective>
Re-capture the committed baselines from the final engine, green every standing gate, and hand all fourteen UAT rows to the artist.

The phase's last red evidence is a baseline set that predates the shipped law. Twelve of fifteen gates fail because their baselines and several of their invariants measure an alpha the pigment export no longer carries, and four UAT rows describe defects whose fixes now exist but whose judgement belongs to the artist. Everything engineering can decide has been decided by 64-29 through 64-35; the amended census relation arrives in 64-38 and UAT row 12 is rewritten in 64-37, so by the time this plan runs the law is final and written down. What remains is to capture the final state honestly and to ask.

Purpose: a baseline is evidence, not a target. The order matters — law first (64-38), determinism second, twins third, baseline fourth, human judgement last. Done in that order, "all gates green" means the final engine reproduces itself and satisfies every law; done in any other order, it means the baseline was fitted to whatever the engine produced. This plan exists to make the distinction mechanical, and it edits no comparator code so the distinction stays inspectable in the git history.

Output: twenty committed baselines (the fifteen standing gates plus five new visible-law gates), each written from a deterministic, bar-passing capture with a supersession note; a full green probe run; and a blocking-human checkpoint that walks the artist through rows 1-14 with row 12 already rewritten to the no-row mixer ruling.
</objective>
