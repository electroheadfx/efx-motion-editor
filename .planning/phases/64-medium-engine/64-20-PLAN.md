Closes 64-17 truth 4 — "Body 60/30 keep the matrix honest and no body value raises covered paper-white" (GAP 4). The verification captured `coveredPaperWhitePct` 0.88 against the committed 0 and the hard invariant `coveredPaperWhitePct !== 0`.

Purpose: the coverage channel is a committed alpha in [0, 1] with no keep factor, by design — a dried near-white film keeps its opacity. But that means it also keeps MOVING while its film settles: `advectInkFS` multiplies the mobile pigment by `uKeep` and `settleFS` moves the rest into `fixed`, while `advectCoverFS` has no such counterpart, so the coverage front outruns its film's hiding-power front and the scan finds texels that claim coverage while the sheet still reads bare paper. The fix is not a keep factor (it would decay a dried film's opacity) and not a second scattering law: it is the scan's own criterion, enforced locally. A texel's coverage transport is scaled by the display's own reconstruction of the combined film, so coverage may only move where the film moves the sheet off the paper-white line. The deposit is already clean — the committed baseline measured 0 before 64-14's advection — so freezing transport in paper-white texels makes `coveredPaperWhitePct` 0 by construction.
Output: the offender census on the white-over-blue report, the `filmPresence` transport gate in `advectCoverFS` with its Engine bindings and source pins, and the probe matrix with supersession notes on every baseline the model change moves.
