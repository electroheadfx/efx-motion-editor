<objective>
Give the coverage channel the same lifecycle the painted channels already have: advect it with the ink while the paper is wet, carry it through undo/redo and whole-document transport, and destroy it with Lift.

Purpose: Spike 001 shipped the model but declared three bounded limitations — coverage does not advect with the ink, it is not in the undo history, and Lift does not reduce it. Each is a user-visible defect if shipped: a covering stroke would stay rigidly registered while its pigment blooms, an undo of a covering stroke would restore the pigment but leave stale alpha (the exact `strokeOpacity` failure mode the Phase 05.2 recall warns about), and Lift would peel paint while leaving an opaque film behind. This plan closes all three inside the engine, before 64-15 removes the app-side punch.

Ordering: 64-17 landed the probe's pigment digest, the `watercolor-50` (1080²) identity baseline and the scenario-keyed invariant dispatch before this plan — its identity re-proofs consume those artifacts, and its new probe scenarios reuse the drive cadence 64-17 added (per-entry `settle`, periodic and trailing fix). Do not re-create any of them.
Output: `advectCoverFS`, the cover advect pass in `step()`, the fifth LayerId / pool-0 format / snapshot entry / travel mapping, the WholeDoc `cover` layer, the Lift coverage draw, `coverageLifecycle.test.ts`, and the `cover-advect` and `cover-lift` probe gates with committed baselines.
</objective>
