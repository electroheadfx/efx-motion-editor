<objective>
Make the sheet cache's script identity exact, and make the wetness invariant able to see a collapse.

This is the fourth of four gap-closure plans from the 2026-11-22 re-review and `64-VERIFICATION.md`, and it folds the two info findings the artist's G-01 ruling kept in scope. IN-05: `seedSignature` hashes only `events.length`, each event's `mutationId` and each event's `pts.length`. A script edit that keeps those three counts — a moved point, a changed width or flow, a different colour, a changed texture rotation, a remapped play frame, or a Dry event's tick — leaves the signature identical, so `restoreCachedSheet` uploads the OLD sheet for the NEW script. IN-07: `PICKUP_MIN_DELTA = 1` passes a pickup response of 49 versus 50 even though the recorded response is a ~200-luma gap; the invariant cannot see the feature collapse.

Purpose: the KEY = CACHE law replaced stroke replay with an exact sheet upload, which is why CR-01's light keying and MED-38's replay determinism depend on the cache key being the script's true identity. Counts are an accident of the recorder, not an identity. The same theme runs through IN-07: a gate whose bar is a thousandth of the measured effect cannot tell the effect from noise. Both fixes are small and both make an existing guarantee honest — a canonical whole-event digest, and a floor derived from the recorded gap.

Output: `aquarelleSheetSignature` (a canonical, order-stable, total FNV-1a digest over every field of every event, with its gate proving equal counts with different payloads diverge and key insertion order does not), the layer wired to it with the old count-only walk deleted, the contract pins amended, and the probe's pickup floor replaced by a baseline-derived bar with a new collapsed-range discriminating case.
</objective>
