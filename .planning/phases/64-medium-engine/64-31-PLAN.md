<objective>
Re-specify the probe's coverage claims on the raster that actually carries coverage, and state the shipped alpha law once, with machine checks.

Twelve of the fifteen committed gates are red, and most are red for the same reason: they were written when the mode-4 export alpha composed darkness with coverage. The 2026-11-21 law made the export alpha darkness-only and moved coverage into its own raster. The gates still read the pigment export's alpha and call it coverage, so they measure a term that is no longer there — several of them now read zero coverage on captures that clearly cover. The probe's own header prose still describes the old composition, which is how the error survived the review.

Purpose: an invariant written against the wrong channel cannot be fixed by tuning its bar. The channel has to move first. This plan moves the coverage claims onto the coverage raster, renames the accessors to say coverage rather than alpha, and replaces the stale narrative with the shipped law plus two checks that would fail if the law regressed. It is the prerequisite for 64-36, where the affected gates are re-captured.

Output: coverage-raster reads in `whiteOverBlueMetrics`, `coverTexturedMetrics` and the cover lifecycle; coverage-named report fields and comparator consumers; the shipped law stated in `probe.ts`, `run.mjs` and `compare.mjs`; two capture-level law self-checks (opaque exports, darkness-only display alpha) with comparator enforcement; and negative gates proving the stale composition is gone.
</objective>
