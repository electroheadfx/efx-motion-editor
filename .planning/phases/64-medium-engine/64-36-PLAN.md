<objective>
Land the amended census relation, re-capture the committed baselines from the final engine, green every standing gate, and hand all fourteen UAT rows to the artist.

The phase's last red evidence is a baseline set that predates the shipped law. Twelve of fifteen gates fail because their baselines and several of their invariants measure an alpha the pigment export no longer carries; the census's width relation was retired by amendment 7; and four UAT rows describe defects whose fixes now exist but whose judgement belongs to the artist. Everything engineering can decide has been decided by 64-29 through 64-35. What remains is to capture the final state honestly and to ask.

Purpose: a baseline is evidence, not a target. The order matters — amendment first, determinism second, bars third, baseline fourth, human judgement last. Done in that order, "all gates green" means the final engine reproduces itself and satisfies every law; done in any other order, it means the baseline was fitted to whatever the engine produced. This plan exists to make the distinction mechanical.

Output: the amendment-7 census relation with its synthetics; twenty committed baselines (the fifteen standing gates plus five new visible-law gates), each written from a deterministic, bar-passing capture with a supersession note; a full green probe run; and a blocking-human checkpoint that walks the artist through rows 1-14 with row 12 already rewritten to the no-row mixer ruling.
</objective>
