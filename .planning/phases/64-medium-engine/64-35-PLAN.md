<objective>
Prove that the texture tip composes with the acrylic bristle gather on the final engine, and pin the texture-axis contract that makes it safe.

UAT row 14 asked for a bristle engine that composites with a texture tip and is at least as good as either path alone. The mechanical part was fixed in `68a7c4eba` — the gather multiplies the tip mask under its mix uniform — but the fix shipped without a gate, and the row's original wording ("the bristle engine composites with a texture tip") no longer matches the mechanism: the bristle classification is id-only, and a texture replaces the footprint rather than selecting the response. After 64-32 and 64-34 changed the gather's rim law and threshold shaping, the composition deserves a fresh end-to-end check.

Purpose: an unverified fix on a moving law is a claim, not a guarantee. The A/B built in 64-29 exists precisely for this check. This plan runs it on the final engine, records the evidence row 14 needs, and pins the axis rules so a later change cannot make a texture select a group or make the tip silently drop out.

Output: the A/B verdict with its readings; engine changes only if the A/B fails; the id-only classification, cache-key and tip-mix pins; and row-14 evidence recorded without touching the row's status.
</objective>
