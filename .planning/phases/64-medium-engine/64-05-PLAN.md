decision: add-alongside
rationale: `light` joins the document's existing render settings — the `background` member that already carries the paper id, grain strength and grain scale — as one sibling optional member on the same paint-layer record, so the change adds a field to an existing container and introduces no new container, record, store or settings surface.

</objective>
