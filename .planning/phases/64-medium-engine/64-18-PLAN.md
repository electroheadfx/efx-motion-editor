Close the light-gesture ledger gap: one drag becomes one document write path and exactly one unified-ledger entry, while the live sheet keeps re-lighting during the drag.

Closes:
- 64-07 truth 1 — "one `setDocumentLight` mutation and one unified-ledger entry per gesture" (CR-02: one entry per pointermove today).
- 64-09 truth 5 — "a same-value gesture produces no descriptor and no panel churn; a real change produces exactly one ledger entry".

Purpose: `SliderStepper` shipped the two-call seam (`onChange` continuous, `onCommit` exactly once per gesture — quick 261111-tul) and every established caller pairs it, but the three light rows pass `onChange` only. Every pointermove therefore appends a document revision and a ledger entry, the applied stack splices to 10 and redo is cleared per move — a single drag can destroy the paint undo stack. The fix must not remove the per-move document write: that write is the only way the live sheet re-lights during the drag today, and the document is the one light state. So the write stream stays and the LEDGER coalesces: the first descriptor of the gesture carries the pre-gesture document, the release closes the span into one entry.
Output: a pure gesture coalescer beside `lightSignature`, the `onLightCommit` seam through the two view layers, the Studio's track-on-write / record-on-commit handler pair, and the regression rows proving one drag = one entry with redo intact until a real edit.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| slider gesture → layer document | A continuous one-field stream crosses from a view into the only light writer |
| descriptor stream → unified ledger | The coalescer decides how much undo history one gesture may consume |
| coalesced descriptor → undo restore | A wrong `before` would make undo restore an intermediate light, not the pre-gesture one |
