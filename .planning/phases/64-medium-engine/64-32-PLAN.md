<objective>
Make the acrylic bristle deposit a mixer with no Body row, and give its rim a real blend falloff.

UAT row 12 asked for Body 100 to mix as a full mixer and Body 0 to mix nothing. That expectation is superseded by the artist's 2026-11-22 ruling: the bristle deposit always mixes with the wet canvas, the artist controls the mix through Pickup, Refill and Feather, and Body is bag-only with no row. Measured at HEAD, the deposit already mixes — Body 0 and Body 100 produce identical digests — so the visible failure is not "Body does nothing"; it is that the stroke still reads as a flat soft bar with a hard rim and a pale halo. The halo's source is measurable in the code: the coverage write targets `0.5 + 0.5·Body` instead of the paint's own delivered alpha, so every dab raises the coverage channel above its pigment, and the live underlay branch re-derives a display alpha from that raised channel. The hard rim is the gather's binary fringe.

Purpose: the no-row mixer ruling turns two stale expectations into one honest law. Body stays in the bag for records and Reset (MED-19, MED-23) and stops being the visible mixing knob; Pickup and Refill become the ratio the artist actually turns (proven visible in 64-33); the rim falloff and the coverage target are what make the stroke read as loaded paint rather than a decal.

Output: a Feather-scaled rim blend factor in the gather with a pure twin and constants in `bristles.ts`; the coverage write retargeted to the paint's delivered alpha with the `uBodyFloor` normalization retained only for wet/lift/smear; the Body 0/100 digest identity recorded as the expected no-row result. The written record of the law — UAT row 12, amendment row 10 and the ROADMAP criterion — belongs to 64-37, which follows this plan with the engine change as its evidence.
</objective>
