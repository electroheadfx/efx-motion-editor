<objective>
Connect the brush groups to the mediums: picking a response card selects the response and, only when its group changes, applies that group's medium through the existing one-write handler — so the Paint-tab dropdown never disagrees with the params, and a pick inside a group leaves the artist's tweaks alone.

Purpose: 64-02 shipped the mediums and their one-write handler, 64-11 tagged every response with its group and shipped the missing ones. Neither can see the other until the pick handler reads the tag. This plan is the one place where mediums and brushes meet, and the last behavioral link of the milestone.
Output: the group-aware `pickAquarelleBrush`, the outgoing-group resolution before the live pick, and the seam audit that keeps every response surface on that one path.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| response card click → live globals bag | A pick rewrites ten persisted medium numbers |
| group tag → medium record | A wrong or duplicated mapping paints every next stroke with the wrong medium |
| pick seam → recorded strokes | A pick must never edit already-recorded artwork through the selection port |
| texture strip → medium selection | Two orthogonal axes that must not cross-trigger |
