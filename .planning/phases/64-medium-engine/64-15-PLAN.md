<objective>
Carry the engine's coverage alpha into the commit and delete the rev-4 app-side cover architecture: record the covering stroke like any stroke, commit the engine's `pigment` export over the cached base through the existing pigment-alpha merge, and let that coverage alpha do the hiding.

Purpose: the punch existed because the sheet the commit consumed carried the darkness-derived alpha — near-white paint was translucent at 0.18, so the app erased the cached base instead. With coverage (64-13) the engine publishes a mode-4 sheet whose alpha is `max(1 − min(rgb), cover)`, so the whole special transaction is redundant: the plan resolver, the per-dab capture, the deposit-footprint shape, the punch into the visible and durable base, the pre-punch ink gate, the display-swap and the deferred reset all exist only to serve a model that no longer ships. Leaving them in place would double-erase (the engine already attenuates and the punch would erase again) and keep four rounds of display-swap seams alive in the frame cache. This plan wires the clean path, removes them, and changes nothing else — the transparent export stays the stored/cached sheet, the ink gate stays alpha-blind on it, and the merge module keeps its law.
Output: `'pigment'` on the engine port's export union; the capture handler eagerly exporting and guard-checking the coverage sheet beside its transparent snapshot; a Studio with no cover plan, branch, handler, dispatch or failure copy; a canvas layer and mount without the cover dab channel; a preview bus and cached-base layer without the deposit profile; a lift-erase module without the cover shape or the deposit profile; the seam contract test rewritten as a no-cover-path contract that also pins the coverage-sheet wiring; and the two cover-only test suites retired.
</objective>
