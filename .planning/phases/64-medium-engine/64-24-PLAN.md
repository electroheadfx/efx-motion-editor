Ship the acrylic's water and its wetness: the document-sized `R32F` last-touch pair, the §3.4 gate that makes Dry stop the mixing, and the D-25 record retune.

This is the fourth and last slice of the acrylic bristle re-opening (D-26..D-29). 64-21 gave every bristle its own reservoir, 64-22 made it deposit the five-nearest carried coefficients, and 64-23 gave the deposit its traces and directional edges. Spike 002 measured the answer: a per-texel last-touch field, refreshed by every deposit and read as `exp(−age / wetSeconds) · coverage`, costs one document-sized `R32F` pair and nothing else. D-24 accepts that cost; this plan pays it.
