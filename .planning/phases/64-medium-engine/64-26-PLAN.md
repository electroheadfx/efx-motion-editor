<objective>
Repair the last-touch field's ping-pong carry and its time-base law, and retire the dead uniform that misdescribes it.

This is the second of four gap-closure plans from the 2026-11-22 re-review and `64-VERIFICATION.md`, ordered criticals-first by the artist's G-01 ruling. CR-03 is a critical data defect: `bristleTouch` stamps only the current dab's scissored rect into the write half and swaps, but the two halves are independent textures — nothing copies one into the other. Every texel outside the current rect in the write half still holds the value from two stamps earlier, so each dab reverts the previous dab's stamps. The carrier and the gather read that field every dab to decide wetness, pickup and smear, and `captureSheet`/`restoreSheet` persist the corruption into the live engine. WR-03 compounds it: raw stamps survive a `timeBaseMs` reset, and the shader clamps negative ages to zero, so a carried stamp can read as fully fresh. IN-04 is the dead `uAmount` uniform this path still declares and uploads.

Purpose: the last-touch memory is what makes a later dab read the age of the paint it crosses. A field that rolls back one dab at a time is not a memory, it is noise shaped like one — and the 64-24 A/B acceptance was built on its signal. The carry is one rect-sized blit per dab, not a document-sized one, because the ping-pong's difference region after each swap is exactly the just-stamped rect; that induction is the whole proof and it is cheap enough for the delivery law. The clock route stores ages at the whole-document boundary, so a bake engine and the live engine can exchange a sheet and each keep the base's true wetness, and the live base never re-anchors under a raw-stamp copy.

Output: two pure, CPU-testable modules (`lastTouchCarry` for the ping-pong invariant and its invalidation, `lastTouchClock` for stamps-to-ages and ages-to-stamps with the precision bound recorded); the engine's carry blit with its three invalidation points; the corrected touch docstring; the AGES whole-document boundary with `captureSheet`/`restoreSheet` reduced to pass-through; the monotonic live time base; the removed `uAmount` uniform, upload, and argument; every affected pin amended in place with the supersessions named.
</objective>
