<objective>
Repair the coverage-composite merge so the engine's own dab law is true on the real 2D compositor, and state the committed alpha law it actually ships.

This is the first of four gap-closure plans from the 2026-11-22 re-review and `64-VERIFICATION.md`, ordered criticals-first by the artist's G-01 ruling. CR-04 is the phase's data-loss defect: `exportCoverageCanvas` writes cover into RGB AND alpha, so the coverage raster is transparent where cover is 0; `scratchOf` white-fills every scratch and draws the source over it, so source-over rewrites the observed red to `255·(1 - c + c²)`. At cover 0 the merge then computes `t_base^0` and replaces the committed base with the sheet (paper white) everywhere the new stroke did not paint; at cover 0.5 the exponent is a quarter instead of the intended half. Every ordinary aquarelle capture with a cached base commits that erasure.

Purpose: the substitute merge (2026-11-21) exists to make a committed frame read like a full re-render — a full-core dab replaces the base, a bristle gap keeps it. With the white-fill inversion the law only holds at cover 1, which is precisely where it is least interesting and most destructive. The fix is small; the proof is the hard part, because the unit gate's `drawImage` was a no-op and therefore could never see it. This plan replaces that blind model with a real source-over implementation, records the RED run, and fixes the read.

Output: the modeled-compositor gate (white fillRect plus alpha-over drawImage) that fails pre-fix; the corrected coverage read (direct 2D read when readable, transparent-scratch fallback, never a white fill); the sheet's white-filled transmittance scratch kept exactly as shipped; the module's stated OPAQUE TRANSMITTANCE alpha law with the display conversion proven end to end over the merged bytes; the corrected comments that called the coverage raster "opaque".
</objective>
