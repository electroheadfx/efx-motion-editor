<objective>
Give a brush stroke the power to replace what it paints over: the phase's one perceptual `Body` curve, then a per-dab cover pass that erodes the settled pigment and the thickness beneath the dab's own tip, scaled by that curve, before the dab deposits its own pigment.

Purpose: 64-03 and 64-04 made thickness visible and 64-05 made the light durable, but the sheet is still absorbance-only — every deposit multiplies the paper down, accumulation is commutative, and a light pigment over a dark area can only render darker. This plan adds the phase's order term, and it is the prerequisite for 64-08's scattering because a covering stroke must be able to erase before it can also lighten.
Output: `packages/efx-watercolor/src/playground/scattering.ts` with `bodyCurve` and `BODY_CURVE_EXPONENT`, `Engine.coverPass`, the guard and protections inside `case 'brush'`, the `Body` globals row, the record-path pins, and the `--body` / `--scenario cover` probe gates. 64-08 extends this same module with the scattering deposit rather than declaring a second curve.
</objective>
