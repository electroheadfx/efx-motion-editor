<objective>
Replace the rev-4 Body architecture — erase the underlayer under the brush footprint, then deposit watercolor — with Spike 001's approved per-dab coverage composite inside the engine: a persistent one-channel coverage target, three scissored draws per covering dab, a pigment-independent `a`, a display alpha that reads the coverage channel, and the `pigment` export name that publishes that sheet.

Purpose: the rev-4 model is structurally unable to make a near-white pigment cover, because the layer alpha is derived from darkness (`1 − min(rgb)`); white's alpha is 0.02–0.18, so the paint beneath always shows. It also pays a full-document `preInk` snapshot and a full-document ping-pong per dab, plus an app-level punch of the cached base. Spike 001 measured both failures (crossing 210/226/246 and luma ratio 0.835 at Body 100) and validated the replacement (241/242/242, luma ratio 0.982, 0 full-document draws, −17.8 MiB per-stroke transient, Body 0 byte-identical). This plan lands the engine law and its source contract only; 64-17 lands the committed pixel gates (white-over-blue, the 1080² identity law, the re-specified wet-underlayer gate), 64-14 adds the lifecycle (advect, undo, Lift) and 64-15 carries the coverage sheet into the app commit.
Output: `COVER_MEDIUM_DENSITY`, a double-buffered HALF_FLOAT_R `cover` target, `coverDepositFS` / `coverMaskFS`, `Engine.coverageDeposit`, the `case 'brush'` branch, the mode-4 coverage display, the `pigment` export background, and the rewritten `coverage.test.ts` seam contract. The rev-4 seam is deleted in the same plan.
</objective>
