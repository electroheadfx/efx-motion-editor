<objective>
Replace the rev-4 Body architecture — erase the underlayer under the brush footprint, then deposit watercolor — with Spike 001's approved per-dab coverage composite, end to end: a persistent one-channel coverage target, three scissored draws per covering dab, a pigment-independent `a`, and a display alpha that reads the coverage channel.

Purpose: the rev-4 model is structurally unable to make a near-white pigment cover, because the layer alpha is derived from darkness (`1 − min(rgb)`); white's alpha is 0.02–0.18, so the paint beneath always shows.
