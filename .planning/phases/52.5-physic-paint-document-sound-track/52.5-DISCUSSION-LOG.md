## Studio waveform surface

**User's choice:** *(freeform, captured during the import question)* Not an audio rail. The waveform is a dark-blue filled stain (same blue as the main app) drawn BEHIND the frame numbers across the full height of the timeline strip; the stain shape carries the sound variations. Frame numbers stay on top (filled, no outline). Tick marks and cuts stay at the bottom as today, unchanged. The strip becomes slightly taller so the numbers sit lower and the wave has room to express.
**Notes:** Deliberate divergence from Phase 15's audio-rail presentation. "The way the main app shows audio" applies to capability, not layout.

## Playback mix & export

| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|

| Clip only; monitoring manual | Studio hears ONLY the document clip; monitoring toggled via the existing Volume2/VolumeX control. | |
