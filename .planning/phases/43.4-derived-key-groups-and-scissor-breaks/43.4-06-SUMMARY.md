## Decisions Made

- The Key Rail is a sibling of `PhysicsPaintLoopClipRail`, not a generalized extension. This keeps the Motion/Static implementation and its CSS as a locked regression boundary while sharing only the existing band geometry.
- The 4px timeline leading offset remains owned by the scroll content. Key Rail projection is relative to the visible frame window, preventing a duplicate offset.
- The accepted rail never follows the pointer. Only the aria-hidden, pointer-inert ghost and existing empty-cell paint preview the retained clamped destination before acknowledgement.
- Group source IDs and frame-override IDs are both excluded from ordinary segment derivation, so one accepted identity cannot produce competing Group and Key Rail targets.
- Stale selection reconciliation compares both stable `firstKeyId` and every ordered member ID. A missing or changed segment clears to null rather than selecting another segment.
