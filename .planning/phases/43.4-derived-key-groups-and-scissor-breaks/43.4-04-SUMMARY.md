## Decisions Made

- The Key Rail selection port contains only `firstKeyId` plus ordered stable members. Frames and display copy are always rederived from current accepted records, breaks, and Group ownership.
- A simultaneously present physical key selection suppresses Key Rail scope; Group Rail selection remains the existing first rail authority, and Key Rail selection is checked before the ordinary multi-key arm.
- Stale Key Rail selection returns `stale-key-rail` rather than falling through to cursor, ordinary key, or ordinary key-group deletion.
- Dynamic scope copy is computed beside Delete availability and disabled reason, avoiding mirrored component state or effects.
- Motion/Static display names are presentation-only input. The classifier and resolver continue authorizing only stable identities and canonical physical facts.
