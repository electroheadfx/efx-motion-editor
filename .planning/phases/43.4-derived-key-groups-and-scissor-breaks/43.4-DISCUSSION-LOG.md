## Final terminology override (user-locked, binding)

One user-facing Rail family: Motion Rail, Static Rail, Key Rail. No `Key Group` / `Key Group Rail` in new user-facing copy; replace Motion Group / Static Group / Group Rail user-facing terminology where this phase touches the shared Rail surface. Internal TypeScript/transport/persistence/planning identifiers keep Group/loopClip names; no migration. Scissor splits a Key Rail; rail selection selects/drags/deletes the complete Key Rail; the destructive action is Delete Key Rail; all selected rails share the orange selection language.
